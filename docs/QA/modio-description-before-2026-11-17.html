OXCE HD — THE CLASSIC, REIMAGINED

Same legendary gameplay. A whole new experience.

Remember the tactical depth of classic X-COM? The desperate firefights, the endless research, the excitement of building your own base?

Welcome to OXCE HD — an independent modernization project for OpenXcom Extended.
