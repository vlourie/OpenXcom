## X-PIRATEZ IS EVOLVING

Imagine returning to your favorite X-COM universe — only this time, your base is alive, your soldiers have voices, bullets tear across the battlefield, buildings collapse, and every battle feels more intense.

**Welcome to X-Piratez HD, powered by OXCE-HD.**

Our independent development team is rebuilding the visual and audio experience of OpenXcom Extended while preserving the tactical depth, atmosphere, and gameplay that made the original legendary.

This is more than a texture pack. It is an extensive modernization project covering the engine, graphics, animations, audio, effects, and game management.

### 🎨 A NEW VISUAL EXPERIENCE

- **HD graphical overhaul** — redesigned and enhanced visual assets across combat, interfaces, environments, and the strategic layer.
- **Improved terrain** — reworked ground textures, environmental details, and battlefield presentation.
- **Modernized interface** — sharper UI elements, improved readability, and enhanced fonts.
- **Enhanced Geoscape** — improved globe graphics and animated aircraft silhouettes.
- **Classic and HD options** — retain the nostalgic experience or explore the upgraded visuals.

Our goal is simple: make X-Piratez look better without losing its original identity.

### 🏗️ YOUR BASE IS FINALLY ALIVE

Your base is no longer just a collection of static rooms.

Watch your organization come to life through animated facilities and environmental details:

- Scientists working inside research facilities.
- Engineers and production machinery in action.
- Animated manufacturing conveyors.
- Casino activity and environmental animations.
- Construction effects and holographic building previews.
- Day and night atmosphere.

Every new facility makes your underground empire feel a little more alive.

### 🔥 COMBAT HAS NEVER FELT THIS INTENSE

We've rebuilt the battlefield experience with a new collection of visual effects.

**Bullets now feel like bullets. Explosions feel dangerous. And destruction looks spectacular.**

- **Visible bullet tracers** that make firefights easier to follow.
- **Directional blood effects** that emphasize the impact of gunfire.
- **Enhanced weapon impacts** and visual feedback.
- **Optional projectile smoke trails** for selected weapon effects.
- **Improved explosion and destruction effects.**
- **Animated structural destruction**, including collapsing environmental objects and trees.
- **Cinematic final-kill effects** for dramatic battle conclusions.

All designed to deliver more action and atmosphere without turning X-Piratez into a different game.

### 🎙️ SOLDIERS WITH REAL VOICES

Your soldiers are no longer silent portraits.

Our expanded voice system includes:

- More than **150 character voice profiles**.
- English and Russian voice content.
- Voice responses for unit selection, movement, and battlefield actions.
- Distinct voices that help individual soldiers feel more recognizable.
- An expanded library of environmental and combat sound effects.

From issuing orders to surviving a desperate firefight, your crew now has a voice.

### ⚙️ OXCE-HD — AN ENHANCED ENGINE

We've gone far beyond graphical modifications.

OXCE-HD is our customized development branch of OpenXcom Extended, focused on improving the game's underlying technology and presentation.

Highlights include:

- Engine performance and memory optimizations.
- Faster startup and improved resource handling.
- Support for additional HD graphical assets and effects.
- Improvements to the rendering and visual presentation systems.
- Expanded audio and animation capabilities.
- Interface enhancements and usability refinements.
- Ongoing compatibility work and bug fixing.

**The original tactical gameplay remains at the heart of the experience.**

We want to modernize X-COM, not replace what makes it special.

### 🚀 ONE LAUNCHER. LESS TROUBLE. MORE GAMING.

We've also developed our own dedicated launcher.

Forget constantly downloading archives, replacing files, and wondering whether your installation is up to date.

The OXCE-HD Launcher brings together:

- Easy game installation and launch.
- Automatic update delivery.
- Management of game builds and their configurations.

- Convenient access to project news and releases.
