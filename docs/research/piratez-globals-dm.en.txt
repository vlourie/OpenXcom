Hi Dioxine! Same checker, this time Piratez_Globals.rul. It's in good shape overall. All 110 commendations use valid criteria, and every one of the 1150+ names in their killCriteria exists. First, a correction to my earlier report. I said minReactionAccuracy works under ai:. It doesn't. OXCE dropped that key entirely in May 2025 (commit "Reaction fire thresholds"), and neither line 8878 nor 9152 is read. Enemies and civilians are covered by your ai.reactionFireThreshold(Civ): 5. For the player's own soldiers the threshold is now the user option oxceReactionFireThreshold, which defaults to 0 (no check). So your soldiers now take 1-4% reaction shots, which the old 5 used to block.
Fix: delete both lines. If you want the old player behaviour, add it to recommendedUserOptions:
  oxceReactionFireThreshold: 5

1) Three HWPs are allowed, but they silently stay at base.
The engine also checks the HWP's ammo (first ammo in slot 0) against the starting condition. If the ammo isn't permitted, the tank goes back to storage with no message.
- Sentry Spike: STR_HWP_SPIKE_ROCKETS only has STR_BAT_CAT_AUX, while every other HWP ammo has TANK_AMMO. Blocked in STR_STARCON_SPACE (6943) and in &allowedHWPExo (7127: EXO, MARS, MARS_CODES). So it never deploys on the space stations, the Moon, Labyrinthus Noctis or Cydonia.
- Red Mage ASC-30: STR_WIZ_SPELL_HADES is blocked in SPACE (6944), GAL_PARTY (4926) and SHADOWLANDS_NOSTUFF (6572). In NOSTUFF you already added the A70's spells to &AllowedItemsShadowlandsNostuff (6566-6571), just not HADES.
- Doctor X A70 in GAL_PARTY (4928): STR_WIZ_SPELL_HOLOGAL isn't in the tavern's allowedItems. So in the tavern only the Vampire actually deploys.
Fix: add the ammo to allowedItems in those conditions (it combines with allowedItemCategories):
  allowedItems: [STR_HWP_SPIKE_ROCKETS]                      # SPACE, EXO, MARS, MARS_CODES
  + STR_WIZ_SPELL_HADES                                       # SPACE, GAL_PARTY, SHADOWLANDS_NOSTUFF
  + STR_WIZ_SPELL_HOLOGAL                                     # GAL_PARTY
Or give the spike rockets the TANK_AMMO category.

2) The STR_NEKO_WITCH_OUTFIT typo from my first report is worse than it looked. In CITY_STEALTH (POLIS_TERMINAL), defaultArmor for NEKOMIMI_H is BOXX_ARMOR, so a witch-outfit neko spends the whole mission in a box. The gnome in grav armor there becomes STR_GNOME_DRESS_UC. Also, two questions from that report answer themselves:
- STR_SOLDIER_DOLL (4681) can just go. STR_AURORA_DOLL_ARMOR_UC is already on 4682.
- STR_DELIVERATOR (4975) is the English name of STR_FIRESTORM, which is already on 4950. It can go too.

3) Two medals count the same weapon twice. Each OR block in killCriteria counts separately:
- SHELLSHOCKED: STR_FLAMETHROWER appears on both 1852 and 1860, so every flamer kill counts as 2. Delete 1859-1860.
- HAMMERER: STR_COOKER appears on both 2122 and 2128. Delete 2127-2128.

4) STR_ENVIRO_MAGMA (7495) leaves Red Mage in Gorgon form. Its armorTransformations is the EVA list typed out by hand, but without STR_ARMOR_RED_MAGE_GORGON: STR_ARMOR_RED_MAGE_EXO. All 12 other hazard environments have that pair.
Fix:
    armorTransformations: *SECTION_ARMOR_TRANSFORM_EVA

5) Confirmation windows use the wrong interface.
- mainMenu 8089-8092: confirmDefaults and confirmVideo are read from optionsMenu, and have been since 2018. The reset-defaults, video-confirm and mod-change confirm windows get the master colours instead of your 89. Move both elements into optionsMenu.
- modsMenu 8180: "- id: button". The mods screen asks for button1 and button2, so OK/Cancel stay colour 133. Rename it to button2.

6) The Coral Island mission counts for Partygoer, not Diver. UNDERSEA_MISSIONS' filter (1095) has STR_SEA_CORAL_EXIT, but that's stage 1, and its nextStage is STR_CORAL_ISLAND. Diary stats record the last stage played, and a mission can't be won on stage 1. STR_CORAL_ISLAND is in INFILTRATION (1088). Move it to Diver, or drop the dead STR_SEA_CORAL_EXIT, whichever you intended.
