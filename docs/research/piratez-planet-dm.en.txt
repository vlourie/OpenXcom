Hi Dioxine! Same checker again, this time Piratez_Planet.rul. It's huge and in very good shape. All 248 terrains have their MCD/PCK/TAB/MAP/RMP files on disk. Every MAP matches its block size, and every tile index fits its terrain's MCD total, with one exception below. RMP nodes and block items are all inside their blocks. In 495 terrain × mapscript pairs, every group and block a command asks for exists. Every one of the 893 land polygons is covered by a terrain condition of its texture. Every zone a mission asks for exists, and all 212 site missions resolve a deployment. Countries don't overlap, and all land is inside some region. What's left is below. Line numbers are from the current release.

1) EMANSION_MAG is missing SPACEVOID (Piratez_Planet.rul:16287).
Its mapDataSets (BLANKS, COMMERCE_MAG, XB3BITZ1_MAG, MANSIONFURNITURE_MAG, URBITS, MANSIONGARDEN_MAG, MANSIONFLOOR_MAG, MANSIONBITS, XCOMEXIT) add up to 250 MCD entries, 0-249. But 21 of its blocks use floor tiles 250 (level 0) and 251 (level 1): EMANS00B, EMANS06_SPAWN1, EMANS_VOID00, EMANS_VOID20, EMANS_VOID_WE00-07, EMANS_VOID_NS00-07, EMANS_VOID_XX00. 250/251 are exactly SPACEVOID.mcd: 2 entries, no sprite, TU_Walk 255, armor 255, the invisible "void" floor, same as in ORBIT_S1B. The engine silently turns an out-of-range index into BLANKS 0, an empty tile. There's no error and no log line. The VOID_WE/NS/XX blocks are the addLine blocks, so the void cross is on every map of this mission. From the code, level 0 of the void should become walkable empty ground and level 1 a hole. I haven't looked at it in combat.
Fix (it has to go last, so it lands on 250/251):
  - name: EMANSION_MAG
    mapDataSets:
      ...
      - XCOMEXIT
      - SPACEVOID
Plain EMANSION (line 15335) shares 9 of these blocks, but none of them go past 249 there, so it's fine.
