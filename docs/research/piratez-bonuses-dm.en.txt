Every 10-level medal lists its own 10 bonuses in order. I compared all 104 medals level by level against their pedia text, and almost everything matches exactly. I also checked the 42 recruits whose starting trait a transformation also grants: all of them are locked out properly, so nobody can pay for a transformation and get nothing. What's left is below. Line numbers are from the current release.

2) ASSAULTER_9 is missing its Silver+ step (Piratez_Bonuses.rul:5307).
From level 5 on, every level adds +3 Reactions+Firing (one "or" plus Silver+ "+1 & +1"). Level 9 adds only +1:
            L5  L6  L7  L8  L9  L10
  reactions  4   5   7   8   9  10
  firing     3   5   6   8   8  10
Other medals with a Max tier still apply Silver+ at level 10 (SNIPER, ARCHER), so by the pedia rule L10 should come out at 11/11.
Now:  _9 reactions 9, firing 8    _10 reactions 10, firing 10
Fix:  _9 reactions 10, firing 9   _10 reactions 11, firing 11
(If 10/10 is meant as a cap, then at least _9 needs the fix.)

Questions, maybe intended:
- FIREKILL: bravery +10 at L8 (as the pedia says), then another +10 at L10 (line 4451, total 20). The pedia doesn't mention the second one.
- WARPKILL_10 (line 4719): energy regen 4. "+1 every other level" gives 0,1,1,2,2,3,3,4,4,5, so L10 would be 5.
- Fractional Freshness drain (FIREKILL -0.08/level, SUCCUBUS -0.12/level). The engine rounds each bonus's recovery to the nearest integer every turn, and fractions don't carry over. So FIREKILL drains 0 at levels 1-6 and 1 per turn at 7-10, and SUCCUBUS drains 0 at 1-4 and 1 at 5-10. It's one step, not "increased drain each level". If you want real growth, it needs whole numbers.
