Hi Dioxine! Same checker, this time Piratez_Factions.rul. It's big (116k lines), but it's clean in almost every way. There are no dangling references, no accidental duplicate ids (only your delete-and-redefine ones), and no zone index outside a region's missionZones across all 2100 mission/region pairs. No weight table ever sums to zero, no nextStage cycles, and every race member is a real unit. The race vs deployment check went through every race each deployment can meet: mission/script raceWeights, base races, retaliation, raceBonus custom deploys, globe texture deployments per zone, and base defense. Two combinations crash; details below. Line numbers are from the current release.

1) Black Bomber with STR_BAD_COPS: crash on battle.
STR_MISSION_HIGHWAYMEN_FLIGHT_1 (line 38811) flies STR_VESSEL_BLACK_BOMBER with raceWeights STR_BAD_COPS 100 from month 0, 60/40 with APOCALYPSE_CULT from month 24.
The deployment STR_VESSEL_BLACK_BOMBER (line 88431) has one row, alienRank: 13.
STR_BAD_COPS (line 25728) has 8 members. The bomber's raceBonus (line 51079) only covers APOCALYPSE_CULT, so there's no custom deploy.
The engine calls race->getMember(13), which throws "does not have a member" and exits. Any battle with that bomber (crash site or landing) crashes: always before month 24, and 60% of the time after. APOCALYPSE_CULT has 16 members, so it's fine.
Spawned by the scripts highwaymenOverflights / highwaymenStrikes (around lines 28095 and 28111).
Fix, pick one:
  - give BAD_COPS at least 14 members;
  - customUnitType on that deployment row;
  - raceBonus: STR_BAD_COPS: craftCustomDeploy: <a deployment sized for them> on the bomber.

2) Black Helicopter with STR_BANDIT_VILLAGE: crash half the time.
STR_MISSION_HARASSMENT_STORMRATS_2 (line 37214) uses raceWeights STR_BANDIT_TOWN 50 / STR_BANDIT_VILLAGE 50, and its last wave is STR_VESSEL_BLACK_HELICOPTER.
The deployment STR_VESSEL_BLACK_HELICOPTER (line 88393) uses alienRank: 18.
BANDIT_TOWN has 19 members, and #18 is STR_BANDIT_RATMAN_STORMRAT, the stormrat itself. BANDIT_VILLAGE (line 25697) has 15, so the helicopter battle crashes for the village half. The bombers in the same mission (rank 13) are fine for both races.
The sister mission STR_MISSION_HARASSMENT_STORMRATS (line 37194) is BANDIT_TOWN 100, so VILLAGE in _2 looks like a slip.
Now:
    raceWeights:
      0:
        STR_BANDIT_TOWN: 50
        STR_BANDIT_VILLAGE: 50
Fix:
    raceWeights:
      0:
        STR_BANDIT_TOWN: 100

3) AUX_XARQUID_GRAPPLE loses its whole damage profile (line 9625).
damageAlter: is written twice in a row (lines 9633-9634). With a repeated key the engine takes the FIRST one, and that one is empty. So RandomType 2, ToHealth 0.4, ToArmorPre 0.1, ToTile 0.3, ToTime 1.5, FixRadius 0 and IgnoreDirection are all ignored, and the grapple uses plain damage type 6 defaults.
Now:
    damageType: 6
    damageAlter:
    damageAlter:
      RandomType: 2
Fix:
    damageType: 6
    damageAlter:
      RandomType: 2

4) despawnEvenIfTargeted on alienDeployments does nothing.
The engine reads it only on alienMissions (RuleAlienMission.cpp), and GeoscapeState checks the mission's flag. Without it, an expired site stays up for as long as a craft is heading to it (the "cheeky exploit" branch).
16 deployments have it. 5 still work, because their same-named mission has it too: CATACOMBS_BUTCHER, CATACOMBS_BUTCHER_ENTRY, HALLS_OF_FEAR, NIGHTOSHPHERE_DELOREAN, NIGHTOSHPHERE_HARVEST.
