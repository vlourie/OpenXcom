## How we make the art

- **The mechanics stay the same.** Only the picture changes: tiles, walkability, cover, numbers and saves are those of the original. At the smallest scale the game must draw a frame byte for byte as the classic does — this is checked before every change to the drawing.
- **Drawn on our own graphics card.** New frames come from local models: repainting after the original, upscaling that keeps the drawing, photo bases. A finished frame is fitted to the exact silhouette of the original, so there are no jagged edges, halos or shifted details.
- **Every batch is checked against the original.** Before a release: sheets of "original next to new", a shape check and a choice between several variants. If the new one is worse, the old one stays.
- **Players check it with us.** The "Art check" tab in the launcher: the frame without HD on the left, the new one on the right, a verdict "good / bad / unsure". A picture two different people called bad goes back to be repainted — the queue is on the site under "Roadmap".
- **Updates come often, in small portions.** Usually several a day; each has its "What's new", and the whole history is in the launcher's "Updates" tab and on the site.

## Done

- **Walls and solid blocks** — every wall has a clean edge exactly along the original silhouette; brick, boards, stone, concrete, rock, ice, base sections — no seams where frames meet. Lights on consoles and monitors blink on their own, not with the whole wall.
- **Ships, UFOs and vehicles** — hulls (about 1200 frames) and the Mi-24 helicopter repainted along the original silhouette.
- **Floors and ground** — streets, sea, swamps, snow, sand, forests, base and ship plating and decks: sharper and without a grid at the tile joins.
- **Map objects** — about 250 new pictures of furniture, devices and small things, plus their copies in other sets.
- **Plants** — trees, bushes, vines and stumps are drawn whole and cut into tiles, without seams.
- **Interface** — battle panels to choose from, radars, a cursor in the colour of the damage, the area of effect, hit chances in interception, high-resolution fonts (a separate mod "HD core: fonts").
- **UFOpaedia** — about 1500 high-resolution illustrations.
- **Environment colours** — missions with their own tint (Styx, heat, magma) tint HD the same way as the classic art.

## Under way

- **Inventory and weapons** — all 1205 pictures are being redone: firearms and melee weapons, ammunition, medicine, grenades. The first layer is ready and waits for the choice on the comparison sheets; then a second, more detailed one, starting with the most common weapons.
- **Walls and ships, finishing touches** — single sets where something stayed shifted or out of place after repainting.
- **Floors and plants** — the next batches, by the maps met most often.

## Next in the art

1. **Ground in groups:** water and shores → earth and sand → forest and jungle → indoor floors, roofs and ceilings. Windows and doors with a strict check of the openings.
2. **Roads:** kerbs and stone texture.
3. **Items in hand and on the floor** — a stage of its own after the inventory.
4. **Units.** Soldiers and enemies are only upscaled from the original so far. Drawn units start with a pilot set; if the pilot passes the check, the most common enemies follow.
5. **Small things:** creature flashes and effects, the remaining jungle rock blocks, posters.
6. **Vanilla X-COM (UFO Defense)** — after Piratez: its sets are different and get a check of their own.

## Besides the art

- **Tactical AI (AI3)** — on by default, a switch in the HD settings. So far it fixes errors of pathing, cover and turn order, without cheating: the AI decides only by what its side has honestly seen. Next: memory of an enemy once spotted, roles in a squad, light and smoke.
- **Building collapse** — optional in the HD settings, works on twenty-odd houses; more buildings come one by one, each checked.
- **Sound** — planned: new soldier voices, Russian first. Not started yet.
- **Launcher and site** — voice rooms, mod builds, the art check, bug reports by F8 straight from the game. And now the history of every update and this roadmap.

## What will not happen

- Rules, balance and the save format do not change. The AI and building collapse only behind a switch.
- The classic drawing stays: any HD layer can be turned off.
- Art that is worse than before is not released just because it is newer.
