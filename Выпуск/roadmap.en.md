## How we make the art

- **The mechanics stay the same.** Only the picture changes: tiles, walkability, cover, numbers and saves are those of the original. At the smallest scale the game must draw a frame byte for byte as the classic does — this is checked before every change to the drawing.
- **Drawn on our own graphics card.** New frames come from local models: repainting after the original, upscaling that keeps the drawing, photo bases. A finished frame is fitted to the exact silhouette of the original, so there are no jagged edges, halos or shifted details.
- **Every batch is checked against the original.** Before a release: sheets of "original next to new", a shape check and a choice between several variants. If the new one is worse, the old one stays.
- **You point, we fix.** Found a blurry or shifted picture? Open a ticket on the site or send a report with F8 straight from the game. Fixes for your tickets come out in the updates and are marked in "What's new".
- **Updates come often, in small portions.** Usually several a day; each has its "What's new", and the whole history is in the launcher's "Updates" tab and on the site.

## Done

- **Walls and solid blocks** — every wall has a clean edge exactly along the original silhouette: brick, boards, stone, concrete, rock, ice, base sections, UFO and ship walls, broken walls and doors. Hills, dunes and slopes fit together without seams or a grid. Lights on consoles and monitors blink on their own, not with the whole wall; house windows are nearly transparent.
- **Ships, UFOs and vehicles** — hulls (about 1200 frames) and the Mi-24 helicopter repainted along the original silhouette.
- **Floors and ground** — streets, sea, swamps, snow, sand, forests, base and ship plating and decks: sharper and without a dark grid at the tile joins.
- **Map objects** — about 250 new pictures of furniture, devices and small things, plus their copies in other sets. Rails, cash machines and much more were redrawn after your tickets.
- **Plants** — trees, bushes, vines and stumps are drawn whole and cut into tiles, without seams.
- **Inventory** — about 2100 items in high resolution: weapons, ammunition, medicine, grenades, armour, documents, parts, treasures, psi items, prisoners and bodies. A clean outline without jaggies, with the shape and colours of the original. Weapons in soldiers' hands and items on the floor are smooth now too.
- **Interface** — battle panels to choose from (including "Ship's brass" and four more drawn themes), globe radars, a cursor in the colour of the damage with a flight arc, the area of effect, path numbers in a crisp font, high-resolution fonts (a separate mod "HD core: fonts"). Screen backgrounds now feature our crew, the six heroines.
- **Living base** — buildings are redrawn and come alive: water, radars, turrets, hangar lights, day and night by local time. When a craft leaves or lands, the camera drops to its hangar and a short scene plays.
- **UFOpaedia** — about 1500 high-resolution illustrations; buildings show the same picture that stands on your base.
- **Environment colours** — missions with their own tint (Styx, heat, magma) tint HD the same way as the classic art; inventory items take the weather tint too.

## Besides the art

- **Tactical AI (BetterAI, experimental)** — a screen of its own in the HD settings, off by default: then the enemy behaves as in plain OXCE. When on, it fixes AI errors: a fleeing enemy no longer picks a tile closer to a soldier, does not hold a path to an unreachable point, searches the whole module when a base is attacked and remembers who shot at it. A separate "AI3" mode stops it wasting shots that have no chance to hurt. No cheating: the AI decides only by what its side has honestly seen. Next: memory of an enemy once spotted, roles in a squad, light and smoke.
- **Building collapse** — optional in the HD settings. Works on hundreds of map sections already: houses, trees, posts. What falls leaves rubble: heaps of debris in the colour of the terrain, felled trunks, brushwood, dust; rubble gives cover but can hurt a soldier. The aim shows tiles where something may fall. New sections are added in batches, each checked.
- **Sound** — soldier voices: every kind of soldier has its own voice; 70 of the 157 voices already have about a hundred lines for selecting a soldier and ordering a move, the rest follow in the next releases; voices have their own volume slider. Footsteps sound by footwear and by floor material.
- **Russian names** — a separate mod "Russian names for Piratez": up to 2500 first and last names for every people and race, each in its own spirit. It switches on by itself with the Russian language.
- **Comfort in battle and on the base** — map zoom with Ctrl + wheel, base-switching arrows on screens, confirmation of large buys and sales, transfer cost between bases, highlighting of matching ammunition.
- **Launcher and site** — the history of every update and this roadmap; the launcher announces new versions and new mods by itself and goes to the tray while you play. Since 22 October: the six heroines of our crew on the launcher's main page and in the site header; the site states which version we play (X-Piratez o2 on the OXCE-HD engine) and describes the mods; the site's main page shows anonymous statistics (how many times the launcher was installed, hours played, how many play now), which can be switched off with a checkbox in the launcher settings; the game will soon show an "Update available" banner, which can be turned off in the HD settings. The "Art check" tab and the repaint queue are gone. Next: voice rooms and mod builds.

## What will not happen

- Rules, balance and the save format do not change. The AI and building collapse only behind a switch.
- The classic drawing stays: any HD layer can be turned off.
- Art that is worse than before is not released just because it is newer.
