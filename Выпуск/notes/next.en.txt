- Soldier voices: the first 45 voices (the whole crew, MERCs, the Church and more) now have about a hundred lines for selecting a soldier and ordering a move instead of 13–21 — short acknowledgements with their own intonation, in-character lines and the occasional joke, so they barely repeat during a battle.
