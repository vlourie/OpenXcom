- BetterAI (experimental): the first row of the HD settings, "BetterAI (experimental) >", opens a screen of its own. Its first row is the master switch, off by default: the enemy AI is then the stock OXCE one and the battle is the same as in the usual game. While it is off, the rows below are grey. "Fixes of the OXCE AI's errors" (on once BetterAI is on): when fleeing, the enemy no longer picks a tile closer to your soldier or runs by an outdated plan, no longer keeps a path to a patrol point it cannot reach, no longer charges a soldier a floor above, no longer fires a guided missile with no route, scans the whole module in base defence and remembers who shot it until the end of your turn. "AI3" (off): it does not waste shots and blows with no chance of damage, does not pick a charge with no time units left for the blow, and goes round a step it was already stopped on. Everything takes effect after the game is restarted.
- The "AI3 tactical AI" switch (release of 14 October) did nothing in the players' build - the AI was the stock one. The BetterAI screen replaces it; the old value is not carried over, and BetterAI stays off until you turn it on.
