- Music player: a small XP-AMP window on the "select music track" key and on the new note button (geoscape: at the right edge by the clock; battle: on the panel). Pause, next and previous track, seek on the time bar, volume, shuffle and repeat, a separate playlist for the geoscape and for the battle. The tracks can also be switched on any screen by keys (set them in Options - Controls; empty by default). A plate at the bottom left names the track for three seconds at every change (can be turned off in Options). The new-message plate from the launcher is now a small dark window with the sender's nick and a blinking wheel. Nothing in the game itself changes - only the sound and the plates.
