- Battle, HD settings, tab "Battle": a new option "Smoke for bullets and rockets without their own" (off by default). When on, a bullet or a rocket that has no smoke of its own in the mod (neither on the ammo nor on the weapon) leaves a light grey smoke trail; it works in any mod and in the original game. Throws, melee, laser and plasma beams and shots with the mod's own smoke are not touched. It is only a picture: the smoke does not affect visibility, hits, damage or the AI (ticket XP-000301).
- Base, HD pictures ("living base"): the water of the pools, onsen, hotel, elite quarters, alenium chamber, well and vats sways more calmly and sunlight caustics run over the bottom, like in a real pool; the metagarden pond has living water too. The hellerium plant is unchanged. Light runs along the pipes of the facilities: orange in the power plant and the reactor, blue in the hydroponics, quiet in the refinery, disruptor shield, cloning centre, body farms and irradiator; once a minute green slugs run from the decoder's tank along its cables. In the labs, instead of sparks, scientists stand at the tables working with their hands, now and then a test tube or a device lights up in them, and every few seconds they step to the next table; engineers in the workshops do the same while something is being made. It is only a picture: the game is not affected.
