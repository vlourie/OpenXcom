- HD interface, paper dolls: the stasis pods of peasant girls, hybrids, lamias, ogres and gnomes are now in HD, all 240 looks. The pod window shows the same HD doll as without armour, under a layer of liquid: the liquid's transparency and colour at every point are taken from the classic pod pictures; the frame and glass are HD.
- HD battle: a new option "Classic shots: laser, plasma, gauss" (off by default, in the HD battle group). When on, shots of laser, plasma and gauss weapons are drawn as in the classic game, with no tracer: a laser is a flat beam with a round head and a light vein, plasma is a ball with a short tail of beads, a gauss shot is a capsule in a double semi-transparent spiral of air. The size follows the weapon's class, the colour follows the weapon. Only the picture changes.
- HD battle: a melee blow (blade, claws, teeth...) on a robot or another bloodless target (UAC security drone, skeletons, slimes) no longer draws a blood spray.
- HD battle: the ground beyond the map edge no longer repeats a craft ramp or other objects of foreign sets; only the terrain's own bushes, grass, stones and logs are mirrored.
- HD battle: in 199 HD sets of soldiers and creatures the dark areas (black armour, dark clothes, shadows on the body) are as dark as in the classic game again; they used to be noticeably lighter and greyer. The sets were recoloured to the classic colours; shapes and edges are unchanged.
- Russian patch updated to 12.4.1: the Zombie Banshee no longer throws away her voodoo attack (other soldiers could pick it up), demon claws take 2x2 in the inventory, the ufopedia layout and the place of "BRONZE + SHIELD" in the armour list are fixed. The per-level achievement pages are kept. Medal bonuses follow Piratez: the assaulter level 9 medal still gives +10 firing, archer level 8 stamina is 10, not 12.
