- The "How many to send to base ...?" window (shipping the goods after manufacturing, disassembly or extraction) has a new "At base" column: how many of each component the receiving base already has in its stores, so you can see at once how many to send. The number is shown only: the amounts, the shipping and the selling are the same. Long names wrap onto a second line. Items still on their way to the base are not counted. (Ticket XP-000374)
- Battle (HD): a new option "Blood colour by creature" (Graphics tab, off by default). The blood of a hit used to be red on every creature. With the option on it takes the colour of the creature's armour: zombies ochre green, the deep ones deep sky blue, ogres and ubers dark red, machines graphite; the tone changes, the brightness of the splash stays. The colours come from the mod's table hd/blood.txt, which you can edit. A machine whose hit shows sparks keeps its sparks. Picture only: the damage, the animation's timing and the course of the battle are the same. Off = red blood as before. (Tickets XP-000366, XP-000367, XP-000368)
- Sound: your own music. Put tracks (ogg, mp3, flac, wav, mid, mod) into the "music" folder inside the game's user folder, next to "mods" (for Piratez that is "user\music"): into the "geoscape" subfolder for the geoscape, into "battle" for battles; files directly in "music" play in both. The game creates these folders itself once you switch on the "Own music" option on the Sound screen (off by default): "mixed" plays your tracks together with the standard ones, "only" plays just yours where you have any, and the standard music otherwise. Your tracks show in the track selection window on the geoscape and in battle under the file name, and the "Refresh" button picks up files added while the game is running. A file the game cannot open is skipped. With the option off or the folder empty the music is exactly as before.
- Battle (HD): a new option "Ground beyond the map's edge" (Graphics tab, off by default). Beyond the edge of the battle map there used to be an empty gradient fill. Now, with the HD scale above 1, the terrain goes on: the commonest floors of your map are laid out in a ring past its edge and fade into the backdrop colour the farther they are. Picture only: the cells beyond the edge cannot be clicked and do not affect the battle, visibility or light. It does not work in vacuum or in a turned view, and changes nothing at the classic scale.
- Interface (HD): a new option "HD interface: window buttons in the battle panel's colour" (Graphics tab, off by default). Window buttons, sliders, scroll bars and arrows used to take their colour from the screen's palette; in battle that palette changes from mission to mission in Piratez, so the buttons in the settings and the pause menu came out grey-blue or brown-pink, and the switched-on ones almost white, out of tune with the window frame. With the option on they are cast in the metal of the battle panel colour chosen below ("Cruiser hull", brass, silver and the rest), like the window frames, on every screen: geoscape, base, battle, settings, pause. A pressed or switched-on button is a lighter metal, not white; the labels on it are dark, on the others light. The text colours in lists do not change. Picture only; with the option off the frame is exactly as before.
- Battle (HD): smoke, fire and lamps no longer freeze. The picture used to stand still while the attack type menu was open, while a unit walked or shot, and through the whole turn of an enemy you cannot see. Now the animation keeps going in those cases: in the menu and while units move always, and in an unseen enemy's turn for as long as nothing out of your view has changed (a door, smoke, fire, an item, a light); once something has, the picture stands still as before so that nothing is given away. Picture only: the turn, the dice and the sounds are untouched. Switch: "Animation in enemy turn and menus" (Graphics tab, on by default); in HD mode 0 everything is as in the classic game.
- Battle (HD): the pools of lamp light on the floors of comms towers, houses and ships look like the original again: a soft light spot on the dark perforated floor with crisp perforation dots, instead of recessed grilles with a rim. The tone and the diamond match the neighbouring plain floor, so there is no visible seam. The same frames in the other floor sets (gold floor, freighter, corporation, ninja) are fixed too - 9 pictures in all.
- Pedia (HD): 26 pictures redrawn after review. The heroines (Aurora, the pirate girls, the Alabaster Queen, the Air Dictator and others) have young faces again, as in the drawings. The underwear is as in the original: gold for the Alabaster Queen, white for the singer, purple fur for the dancer, a light-blue band for Aurora in the ice and as a prisoner; the three warriors of "Pirate Traditions" wear gold sashes, a leather bustier and a light top instead of the same black underwear. The akimbo lady's revolver is gold as in the drawing, and the beast at her feet is no longer a dinosaur. Where normal mode showed nudity (arm wrestling, "Beastmaster", the Lokk scout), there is now underwear or a top. The pirate's cannon smokes, the flag has a skull, the Loknar assassin has her gold belly ornament and more muscle. "Alien Infiltration" gets an HD picture for the first time. In 18+ mode the previous pictures stay; Aurora's big portrait has redder eyes and lips.
- Battle (HD): the game no longer crashes with "Frame(s) missing in 'TRUCK_TURRET.PCK'" when an armoured truck turret turns while the "HD walker shuffle" option is on. Stationary machines without walking frames now just turn without shuffling.
