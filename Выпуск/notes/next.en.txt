- HD battle: the sound of the weather and its life. A new option "Weather sound in battle" (on by default): you hear the drizzle, rain and downpour, thunder after the lightning (the farther the strike, the later and quieter), the wind, the blizzard, the whiteout, the sand storm, the roar of a tornado. In wind and sand storms tumbleweeds roll over the ground, dust devils whirl on dry land, now and then a tornado goes by with the wind, a grey one in a thunderstorm on the steppe. In a thunderstorm the lightning bolt itself is seen. The tornado and the lightning keep off your squad and the spotted enemies. A picture and sound only: tiles, units, visibility and the game's rules are not touched.
- HD battle: weather. A new option "Weather in battle" (on by default, in the HD graphics group): a battle on open ground may have drizzle, rain, a downpour or a thunderstorm with lightning, fog, snowflakes, snowfall, a blizzard or a whiteout, wind or a sand storm, picked by the map's ground: no snow in the desert, no sand storm in the snow, snow instead of rain on the cold maps, nothing underground, under water or in space; about half of the battles are clear. The weather swells and fades with the turns; rain wets the floor, leaves puddles and rings of drops, snow piles up on the open floor; nothing falls under a roof. Far from your units the weather hides the picture like a veil, but a spotted enemy and the cursor are always seen through it. A picture only: visibility, the time units, the save and the game's rules are not touched.
