- Base, workshop (option "Workshop: delivery to other bases", off by default): the Delivery button of a disassembly order with several kinds of products now works per kind - after choosing a base you pick how many pieces of each kind go there (for example scrap to one base, wires and couplings to another). The order screen shows "For delivery: N of M pcs", the delivery plan lists the kinds and amounts per base, and when the order is done exactly the picked pieces are shipped by the usual transfer. Lowering the number of orders trims the plan from the last base. Single-kind orders, prices, times and production are unchanged.
- HD: screens draw faster - at 1920×1080 a geoscape frame takes 4.7 → 2.5 ms, the base 3.7 → 2.5, the battle 2.8 → 1.3, and the first frame of the base with its crew figures 0.4 → 0.2 s; on a weak dual-core PC the geoscape goes 5.5 → 4.1 ms. The picture is the same to the pixel.
- Launcher 0.3.20: updates install faster - each downloaded file is checked once instead of again before installing, and a first install no longer searches a list of thousands of files for every download. The launcher window no longer stalls while checking for a release or redrawing (the "is the game running" check is now light). Later updates of the launcher itself come as one compressed file - about 14 MB instead of 38. A launcher update checks every new file against the verified one before replacing it; a broken resumed download starts over instead of stopping the update; a mod.io game archive in an unknown compression gives a clear error instead of a crash.
- Battle, HD: the dark desert sand (bandit saloon and other Wild West maps) no longer has big golden blots - fine dark sand with specks, as in the original.
- Battle, HD: the energy shield flash of armour (for example the Triarii) is no longer drawn in blocks - the edge of the glow on the soldier and on the item in hand is now smooth (ticket XP-000315).
- Collapse now works on 15 more map blocks. 5 blocks with trees - swamps and wastes, the dead plains, the Coral Island, the wooded junkyard: a felled tree no longer leaves its crown hanging in the air. 10 buildings - the mage's villa, the cultists, the desert, a village, the city, the war-torn city, a junkyard, the Winter Palace wall: whatever stood on destroyed supports comes down. Counts in battles started after the update.
- Ufopaedia, weapon article (option "Ufopaedia: weapon mode range"): the accuracy, TU and range figures now stand exactly under their ACC., TU and RANGE headers - the headers moved four points right, to the start of the columns (ticket XP-000338).
