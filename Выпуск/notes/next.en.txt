- HD interface, dolls: new bodies, faces and hair for human soldiers (peasants and damsels, 128 looks). On the battle panel the face is in HD for nearly all of them, whatever armor they wear. On the soldier's sheet HD is for now only without armor (the three nude variants); in other armors the doll stays as it was until their layers are drawn. Looks with an item not yet in HD (glasses, beret, headscarf, a doll in hand, necklace, armbands) also stay as they were for now.
- HD interface: a "New message" plate - a new option in the HD menu, "Interface" section (on by default). When the launcher gets a private message or a mention while the game runs, a small plate with the sender and the start of the text shows for 6 seconds in the top right corner (Cyrillic and Latin, a long line is cut with dots) and goes away by itself. Without the launcher it never shows; it takes no mouse or keyboard input and does not touch the game itself.
- Launcher, "Chat": the text channels (#общий, #english, #помощь and your own rooms), the same as on the site. The page has three columns, IRC style: channels, "My rooms" and private conversations with their unread counts on the left; the topic, the feed of lines "12:34 <nick> text" and the input in the middle; who is here on the right (@ for the owner or staff, + for those in voice now). Voice is the "Join voice" button under the feed, with the sound and key settings next to it; open rooms and requests to come in are under "Rooms and people". /join, /part, /who and /help work in the launcher itself; /me, /topic, /msg and the room owner's /kick, /ban, /mute go to the site; an unknown command is never sent as a message. Links in messages are not clickable. The feed refreshes every 3 seconds while the page is open; the channels and counters every minute. xpiratez:// links to public channels (with a dash in the name) open now.
- Launcher, in-game plate: besides private messages and mentions there is a checkbox in "Settings" - "Show the in-game plate for every message of the channels too" (off by default). An old conversation never raises the plate, only new lines while the game runs.
- Launcher, top line on the home page: the launcher update comes first and stays, the other notices change one by one every 5 seconds.
- Base, research: with the "Approximate research term" option the term no longer hides under the order arrows in the lab: the count column moved left, progress sits at the right edge, a long name wraps to a new line. Without the option the screen is unchanged.
- Setup from the site: its file properties now carry a link to the official site, and the file no longer calls itself a library, xp-bootstrap.dll (the same for the launcher). This does not change how the setup works.
