- Interface (HD): new HD option "Craft crew: grey rows and seats" (Interface tab, off by default, as before). On the crew assignment screen the soldiers for whom the craft has no room left, or who hit the craft's limit of soldiers and vehicles, are shown as grey rows and the "Not enough space" window does not pop up - a click on a grey row does nothing. At the bottom the "Sort by" and "OK" buttons are shorter, and between them stands a plate "seats used/all seats", for example 12/14, which cannot be pressed. The rules of the assignment are unchanged (ticket XP-000173).
- Battle (HD): new HD option "Shake on collapse" (Physics section, off by default). When a building collapses in sight, the screen shakes for a moment - harder and longer the more fell. Picture only, the battle does not change; works together with "Building collapse", no shake in gentle mode.
- Battle: in a collapse a large 2x2 vehicle (tanks and the like) takes one blow of the rubble - the hardest of the heaps that fell on it - instead of a blow from each of its four cells.
