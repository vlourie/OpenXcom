- HD battle, new option "Footprints in snow" (off by default, in the HD graphics group): in a battle on snow every step of a walking unit leaves a cold grey-blue dent - a boot, a bare foot, a paw, the ruts of wheels or tracks (big 2x2 units on each of their four tiles) - only on snow itself - not on metal, asphalt, roads, floors inside buildings, water or lava; the dents fade over three turns and are gone, and only units you see leave them. It works apart from "Bloody footprints". Only the picture changes: visibility, time units, the turn order, the save and the game's rules are not affected.
- HD inventory: on the "Gear backdrop" picture, the shut slots (Thigh, Belt and the like) are no longer drawn as solid dark blue blocks in underwater missions: the picture shows there, as in other missions. For units without hands, the shut hand slots are no longer black blocks either. Only the picture changes.
- HD battle, "Classic shots": laser beams, plasma balls and gauss shots no longer leave a coloured vapor trail, so these shots now look as in the classic game. Only the picture changes.
- HD battle, "Blood colour by creature": the Baron of Hell, the Aquaman and the Eridians now bleed green. Only the picture changes.
- Choosing parts of a finished production order to send to a base: the mouse wheel over the arrows now changes the number, and holding an arrow repeats the change, as in the transfer window. Convenience only (ticket XP-000561).
- Own music (the music folder): if the game closes silently while one of your tracks plays, at the next start that track is put to user/music_skip.txt and does not play any more; the log gets an "Own music: '...' skipped" line. To try the track again, delete its line from music_skip.txt (in the music selection window, use the refresh button). The choice of tracks and the battle itself do not change.
- HD "Living base", the repulsion shield: the violet dots at the ends of the squares now blink visibly - every 50 seconds three flashes of 2 seconds with a 1 second pause between them: the dots flare up lilac, brighter than usual and with a soft glow around them, instead of dimming. Only the base picture changes.
- HD interface, paper dolls: helmets, hoods, hats and headscarves (32 items) no longer have a black halo around them, and hair no longer pokes out from under them. Dark items stay as they were. Only the picture changes.
- Options, "Lazy loading": turning it off now shows a warning that the game then needs about 6 GB of memory instead of 1 GB and may crash with 8 GB or less. The option itself works as before.
