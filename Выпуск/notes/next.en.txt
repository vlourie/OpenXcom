HD roofs fixed: the alien ship roof and hull are back to the original colours without a seam between them; the light roof in the Eurosyndicate city and on farms no longer shows a diamond grid of dark spots; burnt parquet in the rich district has ash in the middle again. Visuals only: tiles, walkability and cover are unchanged.
HD walls closer to the original, about 700 frames in 146 sets: walls no longer invent their own material - the war city has its dark panels with green lights again instead of mossy masonry, bunkers have their carved blocks back; smooth walls (concrete, plaster, metal, panels) are sharper and in the original colours; grilles, fences and windows have openings of the original shape again. Visuals only: tiles, walkability, cover and destructibility are unchanged.
More HD roofs and floors without a per-tile grid: the corrugated roof in the slums is corrugated again instead of flat asphalt; the grey slum roof has no riveted plate on every tile; concrete in the catacombs and the arsenal has no pile of stones on every tile; the silver roof has straight ribs that no longer bend at the seams; metal floors of ships and stations have fine rivets and grating as in the original, without invented plates and cyan lines. The red vehicle hull and the silver station roof have straight continuous ribs again, without a bulge on every tile. The light tile floor in houses (the arsenal pool and similar) has small square tiles with straight joints again, as in the original, instead of large plates with wavy joints. Visuals only: tiles, walkability and cover are unchanged.
Three more HD floors as in the original: glowing floor plates in the alien base and on Mars blink as intended - the same plate in both phases, only the light changes, instead of two different pictures (an orange blotch and bulging honeycomb); the deck of the Avenger and of stations is striped decking again instead of sand ripples and a circuit pattern; the dark floor of slum warehouses is made of plates with a grid of joints again instead of riveted ribs. Visuals only: tiles, walkability and cover are unchanged.
