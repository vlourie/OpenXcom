- Laboratory with the term option: the mouse wheel assigns scientists right on the count column again, not in the empty space to its right.
- Pilot voices on the globe: each soldier type now has its own voice, the same as in battle. The one at the controls or at the wheel answers.
- HD interface, dolls: new bodies, faces and hair for the Lokk'Naar (16 looks). On the battle panel the face is in HD for all of them, whatever armor they wear. On the soldier's sheet HD is for now only without armor and in Synthmetal; in other armor the doll stays as it was until layers are drawn for it.
- HD interface, dolls: on the soldier's sheet the Juggernaut and DOOM 2 in all colours are now in HD.
- HD interface, dolls: on the soldier's sheet the Ganger, Rags, Heavy Suit and Castaway are now in HD.
- HD interface, dolls: on the soldier's sheet all 145 armors of the main soldiers are now in HD, among them the Annihilator, Assault, Sear, Hover, Harbinger, chainmail, plate mail and bikinis; the Guardian and Chromeback helmets, fishnet stockings, teddy and bra are redrawn; the seam on the Rags tentacles, the streaks on the hoverbike outfit and the faint arcs above headscarves are gone.
- HD interface, dolls: the Ubers' black chest circles are now a separate layer: they appear only where the chest is bare, under clothes the body is clean.
- HD interface: the inventory screen's keys match the modern battle panel: plates in the panel colour and new pictograms (option "inventory keys like the battle panel"). On the Ship's brass panel the numbers in the hand slots have no dark tab and use the font of the soldier's stats.
- The << and >> buttons: in the BootyPedia and the detailed stats screen the previous and next soldier keys now work too, the arrows stay. In the mission diary these keys were swapped.
- Blood colour: pale lilac for the Star Gods, the fallen god included, and green for the Sectopod.
- Nuclear zone: green puddles on the ground are puddles again, as in the original; they had turned into a pale slab with a letter A and stones.
- Jungle: the grass patch with stones has no more black holes and outline; its twin frame now has HD too.
- Swamp and lava: shorelines no longer have a bright jagged crown; the edge is soft and the land colour matches the original. The green lava set is unchanged for now.
- The egg on the floor now looks like an egg: a pointed end, a thin outline, a highlight and a soft shadow.
- Jungle and catacombs: stairs and their sides have a straight edge with no staircase pattern; the red flower and the vines are redrawn, and the flower in the Styx set is no longer cut off; the leafy bush now matches the approved jungle bush, and the bush with a trunk is redrawn to the original's shape.
- Forest and jungle: the dead tree is no longer broken by a strip of grass; the trunk is solid, with roots and a straight edge.
- The barrel from the original is a metal drum again rather than a wooden cask: a straight cylinder with ribs, in every set where it appears.
- Falling trees no longer sound like a collapsing house: they have their own sound. Buildings keep the old one.
- Ship and base chairs are redrawn: a round riveted base, a round seat with a dark centre, a backrest that tapers upward with a rectangular cut-out. The same look in every set, 73 frames.
- The missile rack is redrawn closer to the original: four cruise missiles with large tail fins on a wide stand.
- For mod authors: the engine can now run local Lua mods (battle and items). Off by default, the game without them does not change.
- Launcher, for mod authors: Settings now have a "Modding mode". "Create mod" makes a folder with a working example; there is documentation in Russian and English, an error log and a "My mods" list. Your mods are not sent anywhere; a bug report only shows their name, version and a mark that the mod is local.
