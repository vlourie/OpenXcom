- Fire and smoke on tiles in HD no longer pulse in step: every tile burns and smokes at its own pace and from its own point of the animation, so a blaze or a cloud looks alive instead of a blinking grid. By default smoke swirls twice as slowly and fire a quarter slower. The speed is adjustable: Options → HD → "Fire speed" and "Smoke speed", from 100% (as before) down to 25%. Picture only: the fire and smoke themselves spread and die out as before.
- The "Load" and "Empty" buttons are gone from the craft equipment screen; the button row is back to the original. The X key does their job now: if none of the listed items is on the craft (all zeros in the right column), X loads everything shown while there is room; if anything is on board, X empties the listed items back to the stores as before. The filter and quick search are respected; vehicles and turrets are not loaded by X, use the arrows for them. The key can be changed in Options → Controls.
