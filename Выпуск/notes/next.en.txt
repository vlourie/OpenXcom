- Interface (HD): new HD option "Craft crew: grey rows and seats" (Interface tab, off by default, as before). On the crew assignment screen the soldiers for whom the craft has no room left, or who hit the craft's limit of soldiers and vehicles, are shown as grey rows and the "Not enough space" window does not pop up - a click on a grey row does nothing. At the bottom the "Sort by" and "OK" buttons are shorter, and between them stands a plate "seats used/all seats", for example 12/14, which cannot be pressed. The rules of the assignment are unchanged (ticket XP-000173).
- Battle (HD): new HD option "Shake on collapse" (Physics section, off by default). When a building collapses in sight, the screen shakes for a moment - harder and longer the more fell. Picture only, the battle does not change; works together with "Building collapse", no shake in gentle mode.
- Battle: new option "Battle: a soldier catches what a comrade throws" (the ordinary options, Battlescape section, off by default, as before). When a soldier in your turn throws an item to a comrade and it lands exactly on the comrade's tile, the comrade takes it at once into a free hand - the right first, then the left - for as many TU as picking it up from the ground into a hand costs in the inventory. No free hand or not enough TU - the item stays on the ground as before. An item with a lit fuse is not caught. The enemy's turn and soldiers under foreign control are not affected (ticket XP-000208).
- Basescape: new option "Workshop: delivery to other bases" (the ordinary options, Basescape section, off by default, as before). The workshop order screen gets a DELIVERY button - for ordinary items (not crafts, not random sets, not people, not an infinite order or a sale): pick another base and how many units of the order go there; several bases are possible, in all not more than ordered, and the plan can be changed after the work has started. The units go to the stores of their own base as usual, and when the whole order is ready the shares are taken from the stores (not more than there is) and leave by the ordinary transfer between bases - the same time and price, paid at the dispatch; no money for a share - it stays in the stores. When all is given out the button is pressed in and says "Fully reserved". The completion message tells how many went where; if the receiving base has no room it says "Cap, there is no room there, but the goods are on their way" with the list of bases - the goods are sent all the same. Order cancelled, no money or materials before the end - nothing is sent (ticket XP-000256).
- Battle: in a collapse a large 2x2 vehicle (tanks and the like) takes one blow of the rubble - the hardest of the heaps that fell on it - instead of a blow from each of its four cells.
- Soldier voices: the last 36 voices now have about a hundred lines for selecting a soldier and ordering a move. All 157 voices have them now, so they hardly repeat during a battle.
