- With "Output through the video card" on, the video card now also draws the globe and the base: the radar, the base's lights, crew and craft lights, buildings under construction, videos and the craft launch scene. In the base the processor load is 4-5 times lower (0.11-0.12 of a core instead of 0.52-0.64), on the geoscape half (0.29 instead of 0.62); with the radar on the geoscape is still a little heavier than without the video card (0.96 against 0.82 of a core). The craft launch takes 0.43 of a core instead of 1.06. Battle no longer stalls for up to 0.7 s when the video card ran out of room for pictures. The picture is the same to the pixel; only the glow of a building under construction now comes from a table, and its shade may differ slightly from before.
- Battle with "Output through the video card" on (HD options -> Speed): the battle map is now drawn by the video card, not the processor. Processor load in battle drops by a quarter to a third, and almost by half on maps with a big crowd (the Guild seminar). The picture is the same to the pixel. The classic mode (no HD) is still drawn by the processor.
- With "Output through the video card" on, the video card now also composes the whole frame over the map: the interface, menus, pedia, battle screens. In battle the processor load drops by about half again (the Guild seminar: 0.11-0.13 of a core instead of 0.26-0.31). The picture is the same to the pixel. The globe and the base are still drawn by the processor; they come next.
- Lower processor load: the render threads are now woken only for parts of the frame big enough to pay for it; small ones are drawn by the main thread. On a 24-thread machine the processor load drops by about a third on the geoscape, by a fifth in the base and in battle. The picture is the same to the pixel.
- Map pictures (HD), ticket XP-000343: on the roof of the comms tower (and on craft roofs with the same pit) the corners and edges of the pit no longer have a black tooth, ragged dashes and a dark thread along the white strip - the rounding is smooth and black, as in the original; the same in the silver, black, blue, gold, purple and red recolours and in the station set (80 frames). The lattice cage on the roof (and its copies in 3 other sets) now has a crisp grid without stair-steps or blur.
