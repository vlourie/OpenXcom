- Launcher 0.3.11: "What's new" on the home page now lists every update since your version, not only the last one. Missed six releases - you see all six, by build number, newest first. The list stays after the update, until the next one.
- Launcher 0.3.11: while the game started with "Play" runs, the launcher goes to the tray (the icon by the clock) and comes back when the game closes. If the game ended with an error, a line with the error code shows under "Play". Turn it off in Settings: "Hide the launcher to the tray while playing".
- Harbour trainings: the training confirmation screen can show a line "Closes" - the trainings the soldier can no longer take after this one. Click the line for the full list. With the tech tree viewer off, trainings not yet researched show as "?". Switch it on in the HD options, interface section: "Training: paths it closes" (off by default). A hint only, the trainings themselves are unchanged.
- Collapse now works on 226 more map blocks: swamp, red, snowy and black forests, jungles, hills, the desert, junkyards, city blocks, islands, the port, the necropolis, mummy and lamia villages - broken trees, posts and whatever they hold come down. Counts in battles started after the update.
