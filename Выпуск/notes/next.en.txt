- HD interface: the "Cruiser hull" battle panel is now a dark wine brown matching the ship, not blue-lilac.
- HD interface, paper dolls: Nekomimi soldiers now have HD paper dolls: all 41 armors and 48 looks, with new bodies, faces, hair and tails.
- HD interface, paper dolls: Lokk'Naar soldiers now have HD paper dolls in all 18 armors.
- HD interface, paper dolls: Ogre soldiers now have HD paper dolls in all 9 armors and 32 looks.
- HD interface, paper dolls: Hybrid soldiers now have HD paper dolls in all 19 armors and 16 looks.
- For mod authors: a Lua mod can now show a screen of its own with buttons, lists, fields and pictures. The player opens such screens with the O key or a button in the pause menu; without such a mod the game does not change. It takes luaApi: 2 and luaCaps: [screens] in metadata.yml, the reference and a recipe are in the launcher.
- For mod authors: a Lua mod can now change funds, base stores and staff, hire soldiers and react to a new hour, day and month, and to the start and end of a battle. The launcher warns about such a mod, and a save remembers it was used.
- For mod authors: a Lua mod can now create items and units in a battle, remove items and give them to units. It takes luaApi: 2 and luaCaps: [spawn], the guide and a recipe are in the launcher, and the launcher warns about such a mod. If you remove the mod, what it created vanishes from a saved battle and the battle goes on.
- For mod authors: a Lua mod can now change the battle map: place, remove and destroy floors, walls and map objects, start fires and make smoke. It takes luaApi: 2 and luaCaps: [map], the guide and a recipe are in the launcher, and the launcher warns about such a mod. The map cannot be changed in a base defense. If you remove the mod, its changes stay in a saved battle.
- Geoscape, option "Select craft on the globe with a frame" (formerly "Select markers..."; HD menu, Geoscape, off by default): the frame now takes only our craft that are ready to launch or already flying (up to four) and gives them a common order at once - pick a target on the globe and the usual confirmation window opens, as with the Shift group order in the Intercept list. Bases, UFOs, missions and waypoints are ignored by the frame; with no craft in it nothing happens. A plain click without a frame is unchanged.
