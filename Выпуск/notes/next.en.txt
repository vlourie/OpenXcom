- HD items in the inventory now take the battle's weather tint like everything else: acid rain, cold and heat, Mars, space, underwater, radiation, magma and the Shadowlands - the same as the classic pictures. Before, they kept their normal colours in such battles and stood out. Without weather and in the Ufopaedia nothing changes. Graphics only: game mechanics are unchanged.
- The terrible slowdown in caves is gone ("Pirate Treasure" and other undersea caves): in open parts of the cave the game dropped to 0-2 frames per second. The transitions between floor patches are no longer redrawn from scratch every frame. Speed only: graphics and game mechanics are unchanged.
- The battle no longer crashes when an enemy makes a melee or psi attack on a target standing on a rock or crate on the topmost level of the map (for example, in the ritual caves): before, the game stopped with the error "Melee attack animation overflow" or closed without a message. Melee and psi attacks work as usual.
- The stores, lab, workshop, black market, sell, prison, gym and psi school screens now show which hideout they belong to, right under the title, with "<" and ">" arrows at the ends of that line: they open the same screen of the neighbouring hideout without going back to the base screen. The Left and Right keys do the same (rebindable as the Geoscape "left/right" keys). The lab only steps through hideouts with labs, the workshop through those with workshops, the gym, psi school and prison through those with their own rooms. While the market, sell or prison screen has an unconfirmed choice (OK not pressed yet), or the stores or prison are overfull, the arrows are hidden - confirm or cancel first. The list on these screens is one line shorter.
- Bursts fire without pauses: a long-range bullet now arrives in about a third of a second, and a hit flash plays out by itself instead of holding up the next shot of the burst. Hits, damage and casualties are worked out in the same order as before; a killed target still falls before the next bullet. Both can be turned off in Options > HD > Battlescape ("Quick bullet flight", "Bursts without pauses on hits"); in gentle mode they are off.
- New option "No saves in battle" (Options > HD > Challenge, off by default): like Ironman, but in battles only and apart from it. In a battle the Save and Load buttons are gone, and quick save, insta save, quick load and the turn autosave do nothing. On the Geoscape you can save as usual. Switch it on or off on the Geoscape or in the main menu; in a battle it is shown but cannot be changed. Battle mechanics are unchanged.
