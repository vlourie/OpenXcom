- Geoscape (HD): a flight route of several points on the globe - new option "Flight route of several points on the globe" (HD menu, geoscape; off by default). In the window where a target is chosen for a craft, Shift+click on an empty place of the globe adds a stop of the route (a thin line and a cross show it), a plain click chooses the last target and confirms the order as always; the craft flies the stops in turn with no "reached the point" window between them - the window shows at the last stop only. Any other order to the craft, going home on low fuel, an interception or the target gone drops the rest of the route. The route is kept in memory only and is not saved: after loading, the craft flies to its current point and stops there. Rotating the globe works as before. Off: Shift+click works as a plain click, everything as it was (ticket XP-000470).
- Battle (HD): a burst your soldier fires on reaction during an unseen enemy's turn is now shown whole - muzzle flashes, tracers, the shooter's aim pose and the hits on every round, not only the first (the map picture used to freeze once the enemy fired at died or dropped out of view, leaving only the sound of the other rounds). Picture only, mechanics untouched (ticket XP-000462).
- Equipment screen (HD), "Equipment backdrop": the slots the armor shuts are no longer black rectangles on the backdrop picture - the picture shows in their place, with no grid cell and no dark plate. Without the backdrop and without HD nothing changes. Picture only (ticket XP-000454).

"Auto" looks as before. The icons of the battle panel's keys do not follow this window yet (ticket XP-000460).
- HD interface: the FPS counter in the corner is now drawn in the interface's smooth font, like the numbers of a soldier's stats, instead of square pixels; the "update available" plate under it moved down. Without the HD interface the counter is as before. Picture only (ticket XP-000473).
- Battle (HD): the hit blink of enemy figures works again - red for health damage, white for armor damage, black for stun (Council tip #154). After the action menu had been open a few times in a battle the blink stopped showing at all; it follows the game's own clock again. Picture only, mechanics untouched (ticket XP-000466).
