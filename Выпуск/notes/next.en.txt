— The "Do not follow projectiles with the camera" setting at value 2 works as described again: the camera does not fly after anyone's shots, yours or the enemy's. In previous versions this value followed every shot, like 0. The setting's hint now also describes values 3 (like 2, but the camera centres on a hit on your soldier or a civilian) and 4 (like 3, but the camera does not move at all - this is what gentle mode uses). Only the camera changes: shots, hits and turns are the same.
