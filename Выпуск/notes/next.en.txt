- Screen pictures (HD): they now show our crew - six heroines, each with her own face and her own place: Captain Sever at the globe and the briefing, Iskra in the gym, Shpilka in the workshop, Bazalt in the crew quarters, Rys in the hangar, at the interceptor controls and on the raid alert, Tikhaya in the lab and in the hidden movement ambush. The new painted backgrounds also replace the old pictures still left in places: the main menu, the language choice, the Piratopedia menu, the windows over the globe (new base, course, quit game) and the raid alert; in battle - the briefing before the drop, the turn screen, the hidden movement screen and the battle windows (leaving the map, the experience summary, equipment loadouts, the turn diary).
- Globe (HD): the radar circles of enemy bases and hunter-killers are now red - a crisp line with a soft glow inside, clear over both sea and land. Zooming in on an enemy base no longer slows the game: the circle is drawn on its own instead of being smoothed with the whole radar layer.
- Living base (HD): the base comes alive as in the preview video. The facilities stand on rock with soft shadows. Ripples and glints run across the water of the pools, the onsen and the wells. Radar dishes turn and turrets sweep their barrels. Lights chase around the hangar's landing ring. The hangar floor now carries a landing marking - four arrows pointing outward - instead of a sword.
- Piratopedia (HD): the facility articles show the same painted facility that stands in the base instead of the old tiled picture, so it is easy to tell what the article is about.
- Maps (HD): the rails are no longer blurry - whole, broken and burnt rails, the cart on the tracks and the rails on ballast are drawn anew, in every set they appear in at once (port, cult, military town and warehouses, stations, ships). The TV sits in a real wooden crate instead of a see-through box, and the empty cabinets look like cabinets again, everywhere the same ones stand. The house floor in the wastelands is tiled with stains again, as in the original, not plain grey. The broken red computer now looks the same from its other side as well.
