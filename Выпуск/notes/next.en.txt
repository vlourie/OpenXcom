- HD battle: the "Classic shots" option now also works for the EuroSyndicate laser pistol and rifles, the Space Rangers' carbines, rifle and shotgun, and the Kruger, i-Gun, Haxxor One and Killer Bee: they still drew the ordinary tracer. Other unusual weapons (ray guns, electro dischargers, warp guns, staves, eyes and monster weapons) still draw the tracer for now. Only the picture changes.
- HD inventory: the cell grid under built-in gear on the backpack place (packs, armour modules) is no longer drawn with the black "Gear backdrop" either, only the module itself shows. Before, it was hidden only on a picture backdrop.
- Launcher, the F8 "Report a problem" form: a new "Game settings" item (on by default) sends a fresh copy of your settings as options.txt, with paths that carry your user name and e-mail addresses removed first; untick it to leave it out. The game does not touch your own settings file.
