— Craft and UFOs on the globe are small outlines seen from above instead of identical dots. Your own craft are light blue, nose along the course. A UFO becomes an outline once a hyper-wave decoder has read it, and stays one afterwards, even when only ordinary radar sees it; this is kept in the save. A UFO's colour is that of its crew's race, each race its own. A downed UFO is dimmer. A glint runs along each outline, and a new one is drawn stroke by stroke. The closer the zoom, the larger the outline. UFOs without a decoder, landing sites and other markers stay dots. In an interception the UFO is an outline too instead of a blob: it flashes white when hit and turns blue while its shield holds. Pictures only: detection, interception and combat are the same. Turned off in Options → HD → Interface → "Craft outlines".
— The Combat Training and Psi Training screens now show the soldier's race picture before the name, as the soldier list does, so you can see whom you are sending to train. The name column got a little narrower, so the very longest names are cut a bit earlier. It is turned off by the same option as in the soldier list, "Race and rank pictures in soldier lists" (Options → Advanced → Basescape).
— The articles in the Ufopaedia sections are listed in the mod's order again, grouped by topic, as originally. Alphabetical order is now a choice in the left button below the list — "Alphabetical" (shows the same articles as "Default").
— The numbers above enemies can be set up: Options → HD → "Numbers above enemies". "Blinking" is as before, the number shimmers with its red button on the panel; "Steady" keeps it plain red; "None" removes the numbers above enemies, the panel buttons stay.
— Gentle mode, for players who feel unwell from flashing and jumpy pictures. On the first start, before the intro, the game warns once about photosensitivity and offers to turn it on; the answer is remembered, and later the mode is switched in Options → HD → Gentle mode. What it does now: soldiers and enemies walk at an even pace ("x2" speed), shots fly slowly, the camera no longer jumps after every bullet but glides to where it is needed, the final-blow close-up is off, and quick mode (Ctrl+S) is unavailable. While the mode is on these settings are greyed out with the note "(gentle mode)"; your own values are kept and come back when the mode is turned off. Game rules are not affected: the same hits, the same turns, the same saves.
