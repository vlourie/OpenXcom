- The soldier's paper doll in HD (inventory, avatar, the armour's Ufopaedia article) can now be built from HD pictures of its separate layers - body, head, every piece of clothing - the way the classic doll is built from layers: the HD clothes sit exactly where they are in the original, on any soldier and in any armour. The layer pictures themselves come with a later art release; until then the doll looks as before. Graphics only.
- The HD reticle tells more. Throws and arcing weapons get a dashed flight arc; if the item will fall short or hit an obstacle, the arc turns red with a cross at its end (option "Flight arc"). When the soldier's skill adds damage to the weapon, a "+N" plate shows under the reticle (option "Skill bonus at the reticle"). Artillery (a shot with exploding ammo) and explosives (grenades, mines) can each have a reticle style of their own (options "HD reticle: artillery" and "HD reticle: explosives"). The damage-type reticle colour now works in vanilla UFO too, and blades take it from their strike. Mechanics unchanged; the arc shows the flight without spread.
- The HD tab has a new section, "New functions": things still on trial. It is locked by a password; without it nothing there can be turned on and nothing changes.
