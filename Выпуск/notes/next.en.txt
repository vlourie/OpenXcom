- Living base (HD, facility animations): 16 facilities are painted anew - access lift, hangar, living quarters, burrow, corridor, laboratory, workshop, stores, radar, laser defenses, med bay, prison, gym, onsen, summoning circle and hellerium plant. The base now has day and night by its local time: at night it sinks into blue moonlight and only the lamps, screens and fires of the facilities glow. Lab screens and workshop lights dim when nobody works there, and the living quarters turn their lights off at night. Other facilities look as before for now.
- Collapse (option): heaps and blocks now spill. Crumbled debris lands on the free cells beside them - next to a heap about every other cell, next to a block nearly always - so ruins spread out in a skirt of debris instead of a neat grid. The spill does not pass through a standing wall and does not land on units, furniture, map marks or lifts.
