- "Apple Processing" add-on (version 1.5): apples left on the map after a mission come back to base as apples again, not as apple jam. Puree and jam themselves, their recipes and research are unchanged.
- Blast area in battle. When you aim a weapon that explodes (launchers, rockets) or get ready to throw a grenade, the tiles the blast, smoke or gas would reach are filled with the colour of its damage type - the same one the Ufopaedia uses. The fill is densest at the centre (60% transparent) and fades toward the edges, where the damage is weaker. Walls and cover cut the area just as a real blast does. The area is shown as if the charge landed exactly on the aimed tile (a miss can carry it elsewhere), and only on tiles you have already seen. Turn it off in Options > HD > "Blast area". Picture only, HD modes only; damage and throws are unchanged.
- The Guild Fighter (government and hunter versions too) no longer flies sideways: its HD outline on the globe and in the dogfight window now points its nose along the course. The Pink Ship, the T'Leth Runabout and the ZUBRIN were turned to their course as well. Picture only; flight and combat are unchanged.
- New button panel in battle. The gold picture gives way to a dark panel with every button a tile with a clear pictogram: levels, map, kneel, inventory, centre, next unit, menu, options, end turn, abort and the TU reserve buttons. A button lights up under the mouse and when pressed, the chosen reserve is filled with its colour; the items in hand sit in wells and the unit's name and stats on a card of their own. Works with the HD interface's modern look; turn it off in Options > HD > Interface > "Modern battle panel". Only a picture: the buttons are where they were and do what they did.
- Radars on the globe: choose from three looks in Options > HD > Interface > "Radars on the globe". Circles = the classic circle per radar (as before, the default). Joint area = a light wash with one edge round the radars of all your bases and craft, standing still; costs about as much as the circles. Sweep (trial) = the same, plus waves from the bases at the 5 seconds and 1 minute speeds and a turning beam on a craft out of the bases' coverage. The sweep itself now takes half the processor time it did in the last release. The old "Radar sweep (trial)" checkbox is gone - if you had it on, pick a look again. Only a picture: detection is unchanged.
