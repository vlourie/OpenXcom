- Battle, HD settings, tab "Battle": a new option "Smoke for bullets and rockets without their own" (off by default). When on, a bullet or a rocket that has no smoke of its own in the mod (neither on the ammo nor on the weapon) leaves a light grey smoke trail; it works in any mod and in the original game. Throws, melee, laser and plasma beams and shots with the mod's own smoke are not touched. It is only a picture: the smoke does not affect visibility, hits, damage or the AI (ticket XP-000301).
- Base, HD pictures ("living base"): the water of the pools, onsen, hotel, elite quarters, alenium chamber, well and vats sways more calmly and sunlight caustics run over the bottom, like in a real pool; the metagarden pond has living water too. The hellerium plant is unchanged. Light runs along the pipes of the facilities: orange in the power plant and the reactor, blue in the hydroponics, quiet in the refinery, disruptor shield, cloning centre, body farms and irradiator; once a minute green slugs run from the decoder's tank along its cables. In the labs, instead of sparks, scientists stand at the tables working with their hands, now and then a test tube or a device lights up in them, and every few seconds they step to the next table; engineers in the workshops do the same while something is being made. Scientists now wear dark blue coats, and every crew figure casts a shadow, so they are easy to see on light floors too. It is only a picture: the game is not affected.
- Collapse now works on 69 more map blocks. 19 blocks with trees - junkyards, swamps and wastes, the Styx, the temple jungle, plains: a felled tree no longer leaves its crown hanging in the air. 40 buildings - the cultists, the temple desert, the red desert, the city, the war-torn city, the slums, the island town, junkyards, a farm, the western town: whatever stood on destroyed supports comes down. And 10 government base blocks: when a street lamp loses its base, its top falls too instead of hanging in the air. Counts in battles started after the update.
- Battle, HD option "HD walker shuffle": now you can actually see it. After turning on the spot, a walker (sectopods, reapers, wolverines and the like) shuffles its feet for about a second instead of snapping round in one frame - the turn itself lasted hundredths of a second and the shuffle went unnoticed. Picture only: the turn and time units are unchanged.
- Launcher, game components: a new mod "Enemy Stat 2 - class Randomizer" (by Crazyden85, published with his permission). Every enemy and civilian gets one of five classes when it appears in battle - standard, tank, scout, sniper or berserker - with its own spread of stats, and a few become elite (HP x1.5), miniboss (x1.9) or boss (x3.5); your own units are not changed. It changes the game mechanics, so it is not installed by default: if you want it, tick it in "Settings -> Game components...".
- Launcher 0.3.18, "Settings -> Game components...": a mod you untick now goes away together with its folder instead of leaving empty folders behind. The "Remove?" question has a new tick "Remove completely" (on): the whole mod folder is deleted - with the files you changed or added in it, and with no copy kept to undo the update, so the disk space comes back at once. Saves and settings are not touched.
- Battle, enemy turn in the "As it was", "Frame" and "Frame with a bar" modes (with the HD option "Map during an unseen enemy's turn"): while no enemy is in sight the map stays as you last saw it - doors opening and smoke drifting out of your view no longer show, and the red tile frame no longer follows the mouse. As soon as an enemy shows up the map is live again. Picture only, the enemy turn itself is unchanged.
- Battle in open space (at an orbital station and on a space freighter), HD options "Gradient beyond the map's edge" and "HD unit sway": in vacuum there is no more haze beyond the map's edge, and flying units no longer sway - they hang still, as they should without air. All other maps are as before. Picture only.
- Map pictures (HD), tickets 322-333 chosen by Vitali: trees on the village maps (VILFRNITURE) and in the red, black and snowy forests now have a round trunk and a fir crown instead of a square tower with a cross (54 frames); the double rock in the desert and snow is whole again; the village-road rut runs as one continuous ribbon; the plank walls (URBANWAR/URBAN_GOODS), the well, the petrol pumps (12 frames as one family), the VILBAN stairs, the MOUNTMUD grass and the yellow bush are redrawn; the mutant egg on the floor looks like an egg, not a glass lens.
- Map pictures (HD), tickets 302 and 329 chosen by Vitali: the wheat (CULTIVAT) and rye (VILROADS) fields have their dark edge at the bottom and the sides back and no light grid on the cell joints; rye now has ears of wheat instead of the "roof tile" pattern; the field recolours (green and pale) use the same picture (4 frames).
- Battle (HD): bullet hits on ground, grass, sand, stone, snow, wood and soft surfaces - a bright golden star, sharp clods, splinters and blades of grass instead of a grey smoky blob (42 clips; hits on water and metal are as before). Picture only.
- Globe, HD option "Craft beam" (radar modes "Waves" and "Waves (test)"): the beam is now worked out only where it shows - in the trail's wedge and the narrow strip of the beam - and not over the whole radar circle of a craft. Before, with zoom and several craft in flight the globe's frame rate dropped several times; now the craft beam costs almost nothing. The picture is the same (ticket XP-000325).
- Battle, enemy turn in the "Frame" and "Frame with a bar" modes (HD option "Enemy's turn"): in the enemy's turn the line "Enemies are rustling about" is back at the bottom centre of the screen, and the turn's progress bar is now wide and stands under it, also at the bottom centre (before it was small, at the top right). In debug mode the battle panel no longer slides down in the enemy's turn - it stays where it is, and if debug is switched on in the middle of the turn the panel comes back. Picture only, the enemy turn itself is unchanged (tickets XP-000306, XP-000312, XP-000335).
