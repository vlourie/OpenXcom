- Battle (HD): new option "HD fire tongues" (Options → HD, graphics; off by default). The fire of a tile is no longer four frames in a loop but single tongues of flame in the pack's painted style, bright yellow at the base to dark red at the edges and the tip: each grows at its own place on the tile, curls, tears off its tip and dies away, and a new one of another shape and height takes its place. Tall tongues stand at the sides and the back, low ones in front, so a unit behind the fire stays visible. No two tiles and no two moments are alike. Night vision and water keep the old fire. Picture only: burning, damage and the game's rules are not affected.
- Battle (HD): the "tiled" floor in the jungle and on grass is fixed - tall grass left a dark rim round every tuft, so each looked like a separate tile. The option "HD corner shading" is now off by default (Options -> HD), and grass and other low whole-cell overlays no longer darken the floor of the neighbouring cells; walls, rocks and stumps still shade as before. If the option was already on for you, switch it off yourself if the rim bothers you (ticket XP-000439).
- HD interface: the stack counts of the items in the inventory (the pile on the ground) and the fatal wounds counter are drawn in the interface's smooth font, like the slot names, instead of the old font's square pixels (ticket XP-000424). The classic look and the mode without HD fonts are unchanged.
- HD interface, "Graphs": a pressed region or country button in the left column now writes its name in the colour of its line on the graph, as in the classic look (ticket XP-000432). It works with the window theme on as well: on the light face the line colour is a little deeper so it stays readable.
- Geoscape, the craft window "target lost": the "Redirect" heading is moved up three pixels and no longer runs into the buttons below it (ticket XP-000434). Only the position of the caption.
- Battle: the option "A shot cuts the sound of the shot before" is fixed and described more clearly (ticket XP-000430). A sound the option had faded out afterwards played at full volume and ignored the "Sound" slider until the options were opened; the volume now goes back to the slider. The option's description is shortened so that it fits the options window.
- Battle (HD): smoke and fire no longer jerk back when you close the action menu with the right mouse button (ticket XP-000442). While the menu was open the animation ran on its own and went back to the moment the menu was opened when it closed; now it carries on from where it was. Picture only.
- HD graphics (base): the rail air-defence guns are no longer splayed in a V; the barrels are parallel as in the original, and stay so while the turret swings.
- HD graphics (battle): the lift ladder on the lower floor is now the same as on the upper floor (outline and rungs), with the original's floor grating under it and a straight diamond edge; its copies on other maps are fixed too.
- HD graphics (battle): the "Eight" car's body edge (roof, bonnet, fins, wheels) is now straight lines instead of a staircase; tone and texture are as before.
- HD graphics (battle, jungle): hill slopes are now covered with the same fluffy grass as the flat ground around them - no seam and no blurry green patch; the shape of the mound and its shading follow the original (tickets XP-000429, XP-000431).
