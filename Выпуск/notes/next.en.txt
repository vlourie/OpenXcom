- Reverse gear for vehicles, a separate mod "Piratez vehicles: reverse gear" (switch it on in the mod list). When a path starts straight behind it, a tank, armoured car, bike, mech or other vehicle backs up without turning: no TU spent on turning, and the hull, sight and front armour keep facing where they faced before the move. Moving forward works as before. Your own units only, enemies drive the old way. With the mod off nothing changes.
