— Soldier lists can be sorted by two things at once: in "Options → Advanced" pick "Group soldiers" (by type, rank or craft), and any sort puts soldiers into groups first, then by the chosen criterion inside each group.
— The "Race badge in soldier lists" option puts the soldier's rank badge before the name: the colour tells the race, the picture the rank.
— The soldier's inventory in battle shows stats again, not just weight and TUs: firing, throwing, melee and the armour's energy shield charge ("Shield>95/120"). Without a shield, voodoo takes its place.
