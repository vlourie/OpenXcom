- Interface (HD): the HD options are laid out in six tabs - Graphics, Battle, Interface, Globe and base, AI and rules, Speed; inside each tab the options are grouped by topic (battle graphics, aim and battle panel, globe, base and crafts). The open tab is remembered until the game is closed.
- Interface (HD): when the launcher sees a new release, a small "Update available" plate with the release number shows in the top left corner of the game; it goes once the update is installed. It can be turned off in the HD options, Interface tab (on by default).
- Battle (HD): new HD option "Gradient beyond the map's edge" (off by default, as before) - the empty space beyond the battle map's edge is filled with a soft vertical gradient around the environment's background colour, a little darker at the top and a pale haze at the bottom, instead of one flat colour (ticket XP-000263).
- Battle (HD): new HD option "Bodies fall from blasts" (Battle tab, off by default, as before) - a unit killed or knocked out by an explosion or an explosive round falls to the floor the same way as from a bullet instead of lying there at once. Only the picture changes - damage, death and the battle are the same (ticket XP-000252).
