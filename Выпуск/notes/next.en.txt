- HD interface: a "New message" plate - a new option in the HD menu, "Interface" section (on by default). When the launcher gets a private message or a mention while the game runs, a small plate with the sender and the start of the text shows for 6 seconds in the top right corner (Cyrillic and Latin, a long line is cut with dots) and goes away by itself. Without the launcher it never shows; it takes no mouse or keyboard input and does not touch the game itself.
