- The battle reticle takes the colour of the shot's damage type - the same colour the damage is written in in the Ufopaedia: armor piercing red, incendiary orange, laser gold, plasma green and so on. A loadable weapon takes the colour of the loaded ammo (change the clip - the colour changes), a weapon with its own charge takes its own. The red reticle shown when nobody is seen on the tile stays red. Off in Options → HD → "Reticle in damage colour". Picture only, shooting is unchanged.
- Trial of new radars on the globe. The detection zone of your bases and craft is now a light wash with one shared edge instead of a circle per radar. At the 5 seconds and 1 minute speeds every detection cycle (each half hour of game time) sends a wave from the base to the edge of its zone, one per base radar, and the wave passes on to the neighbouring bases it reaches. A craft flying outside base coverage turns a slow beam, like an old radar screen. A base never waves more often than once per 10 game minutes, and a craft beam never starts waves. On in Options → HD → Interface → "Radar sweep (trial)", off by default. Picture only: detection is unchanged.
