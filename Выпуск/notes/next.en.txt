- Battle (HD): new option "HD fire tongues" (Options → HD, graphics; off by default). The fire of a tile is no longer four frames in a loop but single tongues of flame in the pack's painted style, bright yellow at the base to dark red at the edges and the tip: each grows at its own place on the tile, curls, tears off its tip and dies away, and a new one of another shape and height takes its place. Tall tongues stand at the sides and the back, low ones in front, so a unit behind the fire stays visible. No two tiles and no two moments are alike. Night vision and water keep the old fire. Picture only: burning, damage and the game's rules are not affected.
- Battle (HD): the "tiled" floor in the jungle and on grass is fixed - tall grass left a dark rim round every tuft, so each looked like a separate tile. The option "HD corner shading" is now off by default (Options -> HD), and grass and other low whole-cell overlays no longer darken the floor of the neighbouring cells; walls, rocks and stumps still shade as before. If the option was already on for you, switch it off yourself if the rim bothers you (ticket XP-000439).
- HD interface: the stack counts of the items in the inventory (the pile on the ground) and the fatal wounds counter are drawn in the interface's smooth font, like the slot names, instead of the old font's square pixels (ticket XP-000424). The classic look and the mode without HD fonts are unchanged.
