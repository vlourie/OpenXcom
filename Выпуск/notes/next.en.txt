- Building collapse (the "Building collapse" switch in the HD settings) now also works on three more buildings: the two big barns in the bandit town (the house itself, not the low annex) and the shed on the rice farm. As before, everything an explosion leaves without support disappears in the same explosion, and units and items on the vanished floor fall down. The new buildings count in battles started after the update; in a battle already under way only if it was saved with the switch off and the building is still whole. With the switch off the battle does not change.
