- Gold battle panel. The new battle button panel can now be drawn in brass, as in the original: each button is a brushed brass plaque with rounded edges and a shadow under it, its sign engraved into the metal, dark steel between the buttons, the reserve buttons in red enamel with a brass figure, the hands in raised brass frames with the item casting a shadow into the well, the soldier card sunk into the metal with the numbers in pockets and the bars lying in grooves as rounded tubes. Switch it in Options → HD → Interface → "Battle panel colour" (Dark by default). Only a picture: the buttons are where they were and do the same.
