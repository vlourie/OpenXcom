- Battle (HD): new option "HD volume smoke" (Options → HD, graphics; off by default). The smoke of a tile is no longer a flat grey blot but puffs with volume: each is born at its own place, rises, grows and melts away, so a cloud never repeats. A puff is lit by the map's light where it floats: darker in a dark corner, warmer by a fire, and with "HD flash light" an explosion lights the smoke from inside. Dark soot rises over a fire. The smoke is see-through: units stay visible in it. Night vision and water keep the old smoke. Picture only: visibility and the game's rules are not affected.
- Battle (HD): new option "HD flash light" (Options → HD, graphics; off by default). Explosions, hits and muzzle flashes light the floor, walls and units around them for a moment, in the flash's own colour and strength: a grenade lights several cells, a shot a cell or two. Walls and cliffs cast the flash's shadow; the light does not pass through them. Best seen at night and in dark corners: where it is already light, nothing gets brighter. Works with "HD light" and "HD combat effects" on, in the HD sprite modes only; picture only: visibility and the game's rules are not affected.
- Battle (HD): new option "Bloody footprints" (Options → HD, graphics; off by default). A unit that steps on the tile of a bleeding unit (fatal wounds, standing or lying unconscious; a corpse does not count) leaves bloody prints on the next two tiles of its way, turned the way it walks, in the blood colour of the wounded one (red, or the colour of "Blood colour by creature" when that is on). The look of the print depends on who walks: a boot for a dressed human, a bare foot with toes, a rut with a tread for a wheeled vehicle, a track band for a tracked one, a clawed paw for a beast; a big 2x2 unit leaves a print on each of its four tiles. Which armour gets which look is in hd/tracks.txt next to hd/blood.txt (a name or a prefix with a star, kind: boot, bare, wheel, track, paw, none); without an entry flying and floating units leave none, big ones go by their drawing routine, the rest wear boots. The prints fade in three turns; flying and floating units leave none, nor are they drawn on water, lava or where there is no floor; only a unit you can see leaves them. The prints come only from new steps: a loaded battle has none from before. Picture only: the save, the course of the battle and visibility are not affected.

Picture only.

- Battle (HD): new option "HD unit shadow" (Options → HD, graphics; on by default, can be turned off there). A soft dark shadow lies on the floor under every visible unit that stands on something; it follows the unit and rises with the terrain. Under a flying unit the shadow lies on the floor below, up to three levels down, fainter and wider the higher it flies; an unseen enemy casts none. HD sprite modes only, picture only: visibility and the game's rules are not affected.
- Battle (HD): new option "HD item shadow" (Options → HD, graphics; on by default, can be turned off there). A soft shadow shaped like the item itself lies under every item on the floor — weapons, loot, bodies. The bodies of big (2×2) creatures cast none. HD sprite modes only, picture only: visibility and the game's rules are not affected.
- Battle (HD): new option "HD corner shade" (Options → HD, graphics; on by default, can be turned off there). The floor darkens softly beside walls, fences, crates and everything else standing on it, as in the corners of a room; low rubble shades less. Only the parts of the map you have already seen cast it; an open UFO door does not. HD sprite modes only, picture only: visibility and the game's rules are not affected.

- Battle: new option "A shot cuts the sound of the shot before" (Options → HD, "Battle" tab, off by default). When a weapon fires again while the sound of its last shot is still playing (a shotgun's pump over a reaction shot, the tail of a burst), the old sound fades out at once. Only the same sound is cut; the shots keep their time and order.
