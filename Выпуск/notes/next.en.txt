- Music player: a second click on the note button closes the player. REP now repeats the track that is playing; with REP off the next track plays when one ends, and a playlist goes round. The scroll bar to the right of the list can be dragged with the mouse; a click beside the runner moves it there. The time in the display is centred, and three-digit track numbers no longer run onto the left edge. Nothing in the game itself changes - only the sound and the player window.
- HD globe, new Craft radar line in the HD options (Globe, base): how a craft's radar is drawn away from its base is chosen apart from the bases' radars - Circle (its radar circle only), Wave (a wave from the craft each detection cycle, every half hour) or Beam (a beam turning round its circle, as before; the default). Works with every Radars on the globe look except Circles; the old Craft beam switch is replaced by this line. Picture only: detection does not change.
