- Battle (HD): the ground beyond the map's edge ("Ground beyond the map's edge", Graphics tab, off by default) now goes on as a mirror of the map: the floors of the edge cells are reflected across the edge, up to 36 cells deep, and fade into the backdrop colour the farther they are (before, a few common floors were scattered there as a patchwork of bright tiles). A floor you have not discovered yet is not given away: the commonest floor of the edge is shown in its place. A new option "Look of the ground beyond the edge" (Graphics tab, "As it is" by default) can show the floors beyond the edge in black and white, or sunk in a pale fog. Picture only: the cells beyond the edge cannot be clicked and do not affect the battle, visibility or light. HD scale above 1; not in vacuum or in a turned view.
- Battle (HD): a new option "Border of the battlefield" (Graphics tab, off by default): a line a few pixels thick just outside the whole edge of the map, or a half-transparent red fence one level high along the edge, as in XCOM 2, so that the edge is easy to see when moving units. Works at HD scale above 1, also without the ground beyond the edge; picture only, nothing of the battle changes.
- HD interface: your own colour for the battle panel (Battle tab: the new value "Own colour" of "Battle panel colour" and the row "Own colour of the panel >" under it). A window with sliders: the buttons' colour and the background's (frames, the steel between the buttons, the wells, the grooves) each by hue, saturation and brightness, with a sample of the panel that follows the sliders; OK keeps it, Cancel gives the old colour back, Reset sets the starting one. The window buttons take the buttons' colour too when "window buttons in the battle panel's colour" is on. Picture only.
- HD interface: with "window buttons in the battle panel's colour" on, the buttons are cast lighter, like the keys of the battle panel, and their labels and arrows are dark on a light metal (Cruiser and Consul hulls, silver, brass, bronze, your own colour when it is light) and light on a dark one (black iron). Picture only.
- HD interface: in the battle's Inventory screen the keys at the top right (the menu, OK, the arrows, unload, to the ground) were left copper with "window buttons in the battle panel's colour" on; they are now cast in the metal of the chosen panel colour. Picture only.
