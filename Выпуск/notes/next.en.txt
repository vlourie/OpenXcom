- HD floors: 60 more floor types no longer show a dark grid along the edge of every tile - roads and sea towns, the Styx, alien and USO bases, the sietch, the downed helicopter, mountains, the red desert, the snow farm, the white castle. Grates and tiles with a deliberate grout line along the seam are left as they are.
- Collapse (option): every block with collapse now leaves rubble in the colours of its land - forests, jungle, snow, wasteland, desert, slums and towns. A fallen tree leaves a windfall of broken trunk and branches: it burns, gives low cover and costs 2 TU to cross. Rubble also lands over a stump, grass or a low crate. A fallen floor or a fall of two levels or more always leaves rubble, a single wall or object only sometimes (stone and metal half the time, wood less often). A loaded save gives the same outcome.
- Piratepedia, awards: 20 HD pictures (Easy Prey, Bandit Catcher, Shell Shocked and others) no longer have black ragged holes - the gaps now carry the sky and shade around them.
- Piratepedia on X-Piratez o2: 50 pictures the new mod version renamed (awards, prizes, character articles) show in HD again instead of the classic ones. Where the mod author moved the drawing, the HD picture moves with it.
