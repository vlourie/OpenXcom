— Final blow close-up: when the last enemy on the map falls to a shot or a blow, the camera smoothly zooms in on it, the fall plays in slow motion, the body lies in the middle of the frame for a moment with cinematic bars at the top and the bottom, and the camera zooms back out. The whole scene takes about four seconds, the same for a shot across the map and a whip at point-blank range. A click or any key skips it. Picture and pace only: damage, turns and random rolls stay the same. Turn it off in Options → HD → "Final blow close-up".
