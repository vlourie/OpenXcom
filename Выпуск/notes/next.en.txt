- Battle (HD): "Ground beyond the map's edge" now mirrors low objects beyond the edge too - bushes, grass, stones, logs (not higher than half a cell; trees, walls, hills and slopes are not mirrored). They go from 2 to 12 cells out from the edge and thin out with distance; nothing is shown in places you have not discovered; a strip next to the border has none, so the map's own cells do not cut their tops off. No separate option: the objects come with "Ground beyond the map's edge", at an HD scale above 1; picture only (ticket XP-000398).
- HD graphics: bloody footprints, the next step (ticket XP-000421). Two new rows next to "Bloody footprints": "Footprints last (turns)" - from 1 to 10 turns or "Always" (3 by default; the prints grow paler with age) and "Colour of the footprints" - "By creature" (the blood colour of the creature by the mod's table, whether "Blood colour by creature" is on or not) or "Red". A wounded unit (alive, with fatal wounds) on the move drops blood on every tile it enters - the more and larger the more wounds it has. A tile can now hold up to three prints, and the prints of one kind come in four looks (turned, shifted, a little larger or smaller, worn in places), picked by the tile and the step; the battle's random numbers are not touched. Blood splashes on walls and puddles from explosions are not done yet. Picture only: the battle, the save and the rules are unchanged; with the option off the frame is as before.
- Dogfight (interception): the hitch at the first shot and at the first hit on your craft is removed - the sounds of the weapons and hits and the pilot's lines are now loaded in advance, when the interception window opens, not in the frame of the shot; the window itself takes a fraction of a second longer to open (ticket XP-000392). The rules of the fight are not affected.
- Crew screen, "For mission:" list (option "Craft crew: highlight by mission"): the targets now come in the same order as in the "All marks from our radars" window (T key): mission sites first, then landed and crashed UFOs, then alien bases. Mission sites are yellow, as in that window. Only the look and the order of the rows: mission rules, landing and the crew are not affected (ticket XP-000376).
- Loot forwarding window (after production, disassembly, extraction; option "Workshop: delivery to other bases"): a left click on an item name opens the same window as in the stores - how many of the item are on each base and on the way. The "At base" column stays; the less/more arrows work as before. Display only: the shipment, the numbers and the prices are not affected (ticket XP-000393).
- HD interface: a new window "Buttons' text" (Options -> HD, graphics, the row "HD interface: windows' buttons' text >" under the buttons' look, and a "Buttons' text >" button in the "Buttons' look" window; ticket XP-000399). It has the text tone: "Auto" as before (the game picks light or dark letters by the button and keeps them readable), "Light" or "Dark" - forced: the letters are exactly what the sliders say, blue on black or yellow on white, readability is up to you (a pressed button, lit metal, still keeps dark letters). Beside it: the hue, a new saturation slider (75% by default) and the brightness; the "Text hue" and "Text brightness" sliders of the "Buttons' look" window (ticket XP-000411) moved here with their values kept. Three samples show the text in each tone. Reset returns "Auto", Cancel gives back the previous values. Works when "windows' buttons in the battle panel's colour" is on; only a picture, at the default values the buttons look as before.
- Battle (HD): smoke, fire and lamps no longer stand still while the shot of an enemy you do not see is in flight (option "Map during an unseen enemy's turn"; most often seen with "Do not follow projectiles with the camera" set to 3 or 4): the flight itself used to freeze the picture, now the animation goes on and the tracer over the tiles you see is drawn smoothly. A hit on a tile you see does not stop the animation either; an explosion out of view still holds the picture, as before, so that nothing is given away (ticket XP-000097).
- HD graphics (battle, chimney stacks): the top of the chimney stack has its dark open flue mouth inside the stone rim again, as in the original, instead of a solid stone slab, so the chimney no longer looks bricked up (ticket XP-000379).
- HD graphics (battle, grass on red earth): the grass on the MOUNTMUD tiles (sparse grass on earth, dense grass and grass patches) is redrawn as one continuous field - no seams between tiles, with three carpet variants so the repetition is not noticeable; the bush on these maps is redrawn too (ticket XP-000384).
