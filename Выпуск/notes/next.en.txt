- Fire and smoke on tiles in HD no longer pulse in step: every tile burns and smokes at its own pace and from its own point of the animation, so a blaze or a cloud looks alive instead of a blinking grid. By default smoke swirls twice as slowly and fire a quarter slower. The speed is adjustable: Options → HD → "Fire speed" and "Smoke speed", from 100% (as before) down to 25%. Picture only: the fire and smoke themselves spread and die out as before.
- The "Load" and "Empty" buttons are gone from the craft equipment screen; the button row is back to the original. The X key does their job now: if none of the listed items is on the craft (all zeros in the right column), X loads everything shown while there is room; if anything is on board, X empties the listed items back to the stores as before. The filter and quick search are respected; vehicles and turrets are not loaded by X, use the arrows for them. The key can be changed in Options → Controls.
- The interception window shows hit chances. Above each craft weapon - its chance to hit the UFO; in red at the top left of the radar - the UFO's chance to hit you. It counts exactly what the game rolls: the pilots' firing accuracy and reactions, the weapon's accuracy, the UFO size, both crafts' bonuses, and the halved hunter-killer chance in evasive mode. A dash means the weapon will not fire in the chosen mode: out of reach at that distance, out of ammo or switched off. If the UFO is not researched in the Ufopaedia yet, its own bonuses are left out and a question mark is added ("22%?"), and the UFO's chance shows "?". Turn it off in Options → Advanced → "Hit chances in the interception window". The chances are only displayed, combat itself is unchanged.
- The craft window on the globe (name, speed, ammo, buttons) no longer has the same background for every craft: it shows that craft's own Ufopaedia picture, whole, in the window's usual green tones, muted and darker towards the edges so the texts and buttons stay readable. A craft without an article picture keeps the old background. Works with both the classic and the HD interface.
