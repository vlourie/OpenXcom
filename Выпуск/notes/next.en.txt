- The game needs far less memory and starts faster: soldier voices and mod sounds are no longer unpacked all at once at start, but each when it is first heard. The main menu needs about 0.6 GB instead of 3.8 GB, a battle about 1.1 GB instead of 4.3 GB, and the main menu comes up in 5 seconds instead of 25. Most noticeable on computers with 4-8 GB of memory. The sounds and how often they play are the same.
- The "Update available" plate in the top left corner: a long release number (such as 2026.10.26-16) no longer runs over its frame - in the HD interface the words are condensed to fit.
- Base, HD ("living base", ticket XP-000336): parts ride the conveyor belts of the workshop and the factory and engineers stand at the belts - only while the work goes on in that very facility; the printer moves its heads along the beams, the mint carries ingots and turns the press roller, the forge and the power plant breathe with hot metal, the alenium chamber swells with a soft blue glow every 45 seconds; no more welding sparks. In the casino every table has a dealer in red and one to three gamblers who get up, walk about and sit down at another table; the lights of the machines and the sign run round, the roulette turns. The general stores show a trolley with a load, the tactical center a running line on the table's map. Picture only, HD base mode.
- Base, HD: lighter on weak computers. No more than three facilities move at once (the rest stand, their lamps steady) and on a machine with two threads the base screen is drawn about four times faster.
- Base, HD: the light blinks less. Status lamps burn steady with a slow breath and wink twice every 7-13 seconds; screens change their picture every few seconds (a TV oftener) instead of shaking, the hangar's ring of lights ripples as a soft wave. The hellerium plant is untouched.
