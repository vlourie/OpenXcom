- Music player: a second click on the note button closes the player. REP now repeats the track that is playing; with REP off the next track plays when one ends, and a playlist goes round. The scroll bar to the right of the list can be dragged with the mouse; a click beside the runner moves it there. The time in the display is centred, and three-digit track numbers no longer run onto the left edge. Nothing in the game itself changes - only the sound and the player window.
- HD globe, new Craft radar line in the HD options (Globe, base): how a craft's radar is drawn away from its base is chosen apart from the bases' radars - Circle (its radar circle only), Wave (a wave from the craft each detection cycle, every half hour) or Beam (a beam turning round its circle, as before; the default). Works with every Radars on the globe look except Circles; the old Craft beam switch is replaced by this line. Picture only: detection does not change.
- Ufopaedia: search across all sections from the start screen. The quick search key (Q by default) opens the box left of the title, as inside a section; Enter lists the articles of every section whose title has the typed text, with the same filters. A click opens the article, Esc goes back to the results. Only articles already unlocked are shown.
- HD globe: the bases' and craft's radars (waves, beam, craft circles) are now worked out by the graphics card whole when it draws the HD layer. The processor is nearly free of them: at 2560x1440 the game with radars takes as much processor as without (about 1.2 cores before, 0.4 now). The picture is the same to the pixel. A graphics card without double precision draws them as before.
- Battle weather (HD): snow is now made of single flakes, each with its own size, speed, sway and turn; big near flakes are soft six-pointed stars, small far ones are dots. The graphics card draws it, like the rest of the battle. Drizzle is easier to see (more drops, longer and lighter), still fainter than rain. Clear weather now comes in 35% of battles instead of about half; the other kinds come more often in the same proportions. Picture only: sight, shooting and saves do not change.
