— The launcher now has builds (launcher 0.3.6). A build is its own set of game settings for the same X-Piratez: say, one with HD and your key bindings, another for trying new options. The build that will start is shown on the home page above the "Play" button, and you can switch it there. Create, copy, rename or delete builds in "Settings → Game → Builds". A new build gets the recommended settings, as after installing; a copy gets all settings of the original. A deleted build is not lost for good: its settings are kept in the launcher\backup\builds folder. On its first start the new launcher moves your current settings into the first build as they are, changing nothing. Mods and saves stay where they are and are shared by all builds. While the game is running, builds cannot be changed and the launcher leaves the game's settings alone.
— The launcher has a new icon: OXCE HD over the globe.
