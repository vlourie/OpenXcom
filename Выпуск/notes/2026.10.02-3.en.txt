— The photosensitivity warning shown at first start now says plainly that gentle mode reduces flashes and sudden movement but cannot guarantee protection: both flickering and some patterns on the screen can trigger a seizure. The hint for the "Gentle mode" option now starts with "Fewer flashes and sudden movements".
— Gentle mode: the arrow towards a reaction shooter and the enemy's yellow-orange number now tell no more than the shot's trail itself. The arrow appears only if the bullet crossed the visible part of the screen and points where the shot came from at that moment; it no longer follows the soldier. The enemy's number lights up only if the bullet was seen at the shooter. A shot entirely off screen, or from where its bullet cannot be seen, leaves no arrow — just as without gentle mode when the camera does not follow projectiles.
