A new look for the radars on the globe, "Waves (trial)" (Options > HD > Interface > "Radars on the globe"). No wash: the bases' radars merge into one shared edge, and every game hour all bases with a radar send out a wave at once, so you can see time passing. A craft away from its base has a circle of its own, apart from the bases', with its own wave every half hour. The main base is marked with a star instead of a square. Visuals only: detection of craft and bases is unchanged. The other radar looks stay as they were.
"Apple Processing" add-on (version 1.6): apple puree and apple jam are now eaten with a sound, like an apple. Making jam takes 10 Scrap Metal instead of 1 Iron Ore.
Ufopaedia text lying straight on an article picture (cities, craft, creatures, equipment) now has a dark outline with a soft halo and no longer drowns in a light photo. In both Russian and English. Other text in the game is unchanged.
More HD floors now match the original: jungle temple stone keeps its swirls without moss stripes per tile; the casino and the pirate cruise deck are green floors with specks, no made-up pits; the vampire nest has seamed slabs instead of cobbles; the cannibal camp ground is plain, without twigs; the island restaurant has a dark finely checkered floor; mansion carpets show the original pattern instead of blurry blots, and the dark mansion floor has no diamond grid; the lamia village sand has no pebbles per tile; the Nightosphere and desert temple floors are seamed slabs; the alien base floor is purple stone without orange sticks; catacomb concrete has specks without a grid of boulders; the den's cobbles are crisp; the liner carpet has no little rings; the barn roof is solid planks without a grid. The same pictures went to every set where the floor repeats. Visuals only: tiles, walkability and cover are unchanged.
