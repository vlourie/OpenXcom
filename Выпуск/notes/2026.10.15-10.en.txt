- Building collapse (the "Building collapse" switch in the HD settings) now works on twenty-two more houses: city houses and blocks, houses in the commercial quarter, farms and a junkyard. As before, everything a blast leaves without support disappears in the same blast, and soldiers and items on a vanished floor fall down; furniture, crates and stairs on a blown-out floor go with it. On some houses a floor may stay up after a blast where it looks like it should fall - if you spot one, please send the save. The new houses count in battles started after the update. With the switch off, battles are unchanged.
- The rest of the HD inventory: armor, documents, parts and treasures, trinkets and gear, psi items, prisoners and bodies - about 900 more pictures are redrawn in high resolution, like the weapons in the previous update: a clean outline with no stair-steps, the same shape and colors as the original. Graphics only: weight, grid cells and item stats are unchanged.
