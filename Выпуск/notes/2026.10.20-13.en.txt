- Solid blocks of earth and rock in HD (in forests, deserts, caves, on Mars, islands and city blocks) now stack into slopes and roofs without seams: no grid on top, no stripes on the sides or between levels. The cables on the freighter are green again as in the original, and toxic water no longer has a green rim and blotches along its edge. 23 frames in total. Graphics only.
- Building collapse now works on seven more buildings with trees or lamp posts next to them: four houses of the commercial quarter, a city house, a junkyard house and a slum block. Trees and posts standing from the ground take no part in a collapse - they stay as before until the blast itself cuts them down; a lamp on a wall, a sign and anything else held by the house fall with it. The new buildings count in battles started after the update.
