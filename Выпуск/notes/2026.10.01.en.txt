— The icons over knocked-out bodies are animated now. The ghost raises and lowers its little arms in turn, the blood drops swell and drip down one after another, the Zzz letters appear by the body and float away from it, and the flame sways left and right and up and down. The HD icons over bodies also finally made it into the release: until now players saw the old ones scaled up. Picture only, in HD modes 1 and 2.
— A craft's name now shows what is installed on it: in the interception window on the Geoscape and in the base's craft list, the same green weapon and equipment icons as on the craft screen stand before the name, one per installed item. No more need to encode the loadout in the craft's name. The interception window's columns moved a little to make room. It can be turned off with the option "Craft equipment icons before the name" (Options → Advanced → Geoscape).
— The game can no longer hang at the very end of loading (the text screen is done but the menu never shows up) or fail to close after you quit. The thread that loads the data used to be cut off at the very moment it was finishing its work, and now and then it did not get to release its memory. Now the game waits for it. In play this was rare; on the AI test bench, where the game is launched thousands of times and quits right after the battle in fast mode, every fourth launch hung (4 of 16), after the fix none of 16.
