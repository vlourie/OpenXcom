Two-way exchange between bases on the Transfer screen: one window shows both bases, and items, soldiers, staff and craft can be sent either way. The right arrow sends to the second base, the left arrow back to the first; the middle column reads "N →" or "← N", the outer columns show what each base will have after the transfer. Room in stores, quarters, hangars and containment is checked at both bases, net of what each base sends away. Turn it off in Options > Advanced, Basescape section, "Two-way exchange on the transfer screen" to get the old one-way screen. The after-battle transfer screen is unchanged.
More HD floors now match the original: catacombs and jungle temples have cobbles instead of a grid of even slabs; the X-COM base floor has its rivets instead of a blurry plate; barracks and the cruise liner show camouflage instead of blurry clouds; the slums have a diagonal grating instead of made-up panels; the hull of crashed planes in the tundra, on the ice and in the desert keeps the original stripes and camouflage without made-up pyramids and badges per tile. The same pictures went to every set where the floor repeats. Visuals only: tiles, walkability and cover are unchanged. If you see diamonds, stripes at joints or patches of another colour, press F8, leave a screenshot and tick "attach save".
