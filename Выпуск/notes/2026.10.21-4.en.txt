- New mod "Русские имена Пираток 0.1" (Russian names for Piratez): every Piratez nation and race now has up to 2500 first and last names each (was tens to hundreds), each in its own spirit - bold outlaw Gals, real names of each nation, hissing Lamias, machine-like Synths, short dog names. Turns on by itself with the Russian language, right after "RU: Piratez Cyrillic Names" and instead of its names; that mod itself is not changed - switch the new one off and the old names are back. Only new soldiers get the new names; hired soldiers and saves are not renamed.
