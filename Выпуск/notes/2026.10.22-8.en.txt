- Soldier voices: the first 45 voices (the whole crew, MERCs, the Church and more) now have about a hundred lines for selecting a soldier and ordering a move instead of 13–21 — short acknowledgements with their own intonation, in-character lines and the occasional joke, so they barely repeat during a battle. Characters who have their own voice in the English Piratez (Gals, peons, slaves, hybrids, Lokk'Naar, gnomes, cats, Caleb the ogre, the Killbot, the Xenforcer) now speak in a voice as close as possible to the game's — in both Russian and English. The remaining voices get their hundred lines in upcoming releases.
- Living base (HD): the laser and gauss turrets swing their barrels again, but now only the barrel with its ball moves — the walls, floor and mount stay put, and the floor under the barrel is painted in. The plasma turret fires: violet sparks gather into the centre of its orb, the orb flares up and a blue plasma beam shoots upward.
- Collapse (option): in HD there are now two different concrete slabs in the rubble, tilted to opposite sides, instead of the same slab everywhere.
- Battle (HD interface): the time units and energy on the path's tiles are written in the interface's sharp font instead of pixel digits — smaller on the steps, larger at the path's end, so the cost of the whole path shows at a glance. Can be turned off with the option "Path numbers in the interface font" (ticket XP-000115).
- Battle (HD panel): a small arrow over the top right corner of the panel slides the panel down off the screen to show the bottom of the map; the arrow stays at the edge and brings it back. The panel's hotkeys still work while it is hidden. Option "Battle panel hide arrow" (ticket XP-000114).
