- Building collapse (the "Building collapse" switch in the HD settings) now also works on the rice farm shed. As before, everything an explosion leaves without support disappears in the same explosion, and units and items on the vanished floor fall down. The shed counts in battles started after the update; in a battle already under way only if it was saved with the switch off and the shed is still whole. With the switch off the battle does not change.
- Thirteen more HD plants instead of blurry ones: a snow-covered log in the mountains, a tree in the tropical mountains, vines and a plant in the jungle, a glowing flower and two blue-berry bushes in the cave, a burning dead tree in the catacombs, a bush on the savanna beach, a burnt stump with rust-red leaves in the wasteland, a broken mast with a rope on a sunken ship, a snow-covered broken tree in the mountains and a split tree on the mountain wasteland. Picture only: tiles, passability and cover are unchanged.
- AI3 tactical AI: the HD settings have a new "Tactical AI" group with the "AI3 tactical AI" switch, on by default, takes effect after the game is restarted. The enemy no longer picks a charge its unit has no time units left to carry out; errors in paths, cover and the order units act in, from the AI2 base, are fixed; the AI decides only from what its side has honestly seen (after loading a save too), and from the same battle start it plays the same way. These are bug fixes, not new tactics: the AI is not noticeably stronger. With the switch off you get the stock OXCE AI. Separately: the autopilot of the AI test bench can now use psi attacks and waypoint launches; it never turns on in normal play and never controls the player's squad.
