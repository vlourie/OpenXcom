- Building collapse (trial): the HD settings have a new "Physics" group with a "Building collapse" switch, off by default. When it is on and an explosion knocks out a building's supports, everything left without support disappears in the same explosion, and units and items on the vanished floor fall down. For now only the farm barn and the base defence bunker, with no debris animation. It changes the battle itself, so it stays off until the player turns it on.
