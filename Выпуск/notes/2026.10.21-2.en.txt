- Radars on the globe, "Waves" mode: the bases no longer send their waves all at once - by default they take turns, a few at a time, spread over the hour. New option "Base waves": all at once each hour (as before), in turn each hour, each 3 hours or each 6 hours. A craft out of its base now turns a beam, like an old radar screen, instead of sending a wave; switch "Craft beam" off to get the wave back. Graphics only.
- Radars on the globe: the waves and the craft's beam no longer run while the time stands - with a window open over the globe or during an interception they freeze along with the clock.
- "Production complete" window: above the "Keep working" button it now says which job comes next - the top one on the list, which gets the free engineers.
- Purchase and sell screens at a base: OK no longer closes the screen - it opens anew on the same base, with the same category and scroll, so the "<" and ">" arrows take you straight to buying or selling at another base. Leave with Cancel. Selling after a battle or with overfull stores closes as before. To get the old way back, switch off "Purchase and sale: stay on the screen after the deal" in the advanced options, base section.
- Purchase and sale: a deal of five lines or more first shows a window with the chosen lines, their quantity, the sum of each line and the total - confirm it or go back to the choice. How many lines it takes (1 - always, 0 - never) is set by "Purchase and sale: confirm from this many lines".
- Battle inventory: a medikit, food or pills can be dropped right onto the soldier's figure - the soldier takes it into a free hand and uses it on themselves. A medikit opens its healing window, an apple or other food is eaten at once, a reusable item stays in the hand. The TU spent are exactly those of taking it in hand and using it from the action menu. With both hands full the game asks to free one; without enough TU for both steps it asks not to hurry. Switch it off with "Battle inventory: use by dropping on the soldier".
