- HD globe on the video card: the blinking base and craft markers and the unchanged parts of the layer over the globe are no longer smoothed and sent to the video card again every 100 ms; when the day and night shadow moves, only the changed pieces of the globe are sent, not all of it; the enemy radar circles are not recomputed while the globe stands still. The globe takes almost half as much processor (at 2560x1440 about 0.2 of a core instead of 0.4) and sends 6 times less to the video card. The picture is the same to the pixel.
- HD options, Graphics tab, new checkbox "Classic paperdolls" (under "HD pictures"): the paper dolls of soldiers and vehicles are shown as the classic pictures instead of HD ones - in the inventory, on the soldier sheet, on the armor pages of the Ufopaedia and the face on the battle panel. All other HD pictures, the HD interface and the battle stay HD. Works at once, no restart; off by default. In missions with special weather (dust storm, heat, cold) the classic dolls are tinted with the battle palette, as in the original. Picture only.
- HD options put in order: six tabs by subject (Graphics, Battle, Aim, Interface, Globe and base, Game), similar settings side by side, 79 rows instead of 121. 34 rows merged into 16 (for example, light and flash light are one row "Light in battle"; the battle panel and its colour are one row). 24 rows left the menu: 16 small conveniences are now always on for everyone (Ctrl + wheel map zoom, fitting ammo lit up, the wounded in red, the transfer cost by the buttons and others), 8 fine settings (HD scale, fire and smoke pace, digit weight and the like) stay in options.cfg. No function is lost, the values in options.cfg are kept.
- HD options, first row "HD settings guide": opens a page on the game's site in your browser with a description of every setting, all its values and frames of the game with each; what works by itself is described by screen. The same page opens from the launcher and from the site menu ("HD settings"). Needs the internet.
- Music player without HD: the letters no longer run together into blots - their dark outline is no longer painted in the text colour, only the letter itself shows. The track name plate, the chat plate and the note button are fixed the same way. The player's buttons and windows are unchanged.
