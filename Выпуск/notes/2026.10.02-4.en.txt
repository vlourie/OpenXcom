— Gentle mode: the camera no longer jumps. When you pick a soldier (Tab, next/previous buttons), press an enemy button, during the enemy turn and on explosions, the picture glides to the place in about half a second. If the soldier or enemy is already on screen, the camera stays where it is. Press Tab again while the picture is moving and it smoothly turns towards the new soldier, with no jerk and no trip to the previous one. While the picture is moving (a fraction of a second) clicks on the map are ignored, so you don't hit the wrong tile. A jump further than a screen and a half is still instant for now. The "center on soldier" button always centers. Shots, hits and turns are the same.
