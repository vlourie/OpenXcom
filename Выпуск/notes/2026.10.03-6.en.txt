- Voice in the launcher is open to all players, and the "Voice" section is now called "Chat" (launcher 0.3.8). Link the launcher to your site account in Settings to see everyone's open rooms: ask to enter any of them, and the owner can let you in with one click even if you are not friends. On the right is who is online right now, that is, whose launcher is running: add them as a friend or call them into your room. "Open my room" creates a room and takes you straight in. In your own room you are the admin: let people in on request, take away the right to speak, kick and ban. Microphone, speakers and the talk key are now behind the "Settings" button.
