- Living base (HD): every facility now has an animation of its own. A scanning beam sweeps over the radars, the space radio and the defence turrets, rings spread on the water of the pools, the onsen, the well and the shields, flasks and pods bubble in the labs, the cloning centre and the farms, sparks circle the computer core, the Eye of Horus, the black tower and the nine circles, mist creeps in the gas chamber and the cryo prison, smoke rises from damaged facilities, dust and pollen drift over the stores, libraries, halls and plantations, bees buzz in the hive, flies circle the beast den, the dungeon and the field hospital, and spotlights roam the club and the casino. The crew now walks and stands only on bare floor - around furniture, machines and water - and goes from room to room through the connecting tube.
