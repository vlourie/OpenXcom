- Launcher 0.3.12: when you press "Update", the launcher tells you if new mods you have not seen yet came out and offers to open the install page. "Yes" opens the mods page with the new ones marked "new" - only what you tick gets installed. "No" goes on with the update as usual. The launcher does not ask twice about the same mods. Right after the switch to this version it asks once about every suitable mod you have not ticked.
- Launcher 0.3.12: while the launcher is open or in the tray (the game time included), it looks for a new version every 15 minutes. When one is out, a Windows notification says "Update ... is out"; click it to open the launcher. The "Update" button on the home page shows up by itself, no manual check needed. Turn the notifications off in Settings: "Tell me about new versions while the launcher is open or in the tray".
- Launcher 0.3.12: the window's cross now hides the launcher to the tray (the icon by the clock) instead of closing it, so it keeps watching for new versions. Click the icon to open it. To exit for good - right-click the icon, "Close the launcher". The old behaviour is in Settings: "The window's cross hides the launcher to the tray".
