- Battle: new option "Coloured hit flashes" (HD menu, Graphics; on by default, everything as it was). Turn it off and a unit's body no longer flashes white, red and other colours when hit - the X-Piratez mod's flash script is not drawn. Only the picture changes: damage, turns, visibility and saves are the same; the recolouring of shields, fire and other effects stays (ticket XP-000490).
- HD smoke: the option "HD volume smoke" now has three values - "Previous" (the pack's flat smoke picture), "Puffs" (as it was, with gaps between the puffs) and the new "Puffs and veil": the same puffs over a see-through layer of the smoke picture, so the smoke stands as a solid veil without gaps, as under night vision, but in the usual colours. The old on / off setting reads as before (on = "Puffs", off = "Previous"). Only a picture: visibility and the game's rules are not affected; the veil is a little heavier on FPS than the puffs (ticket XP-000482).
- Base, research: new option "Approximate research term in the scientists window" (HD menu, "Base and crafts"; off by default). In the window where scientists are assigned to a project a line "Time left: about from-to days" appears. It is counted from the project's cost in the ruleset, the man-days already spent and the scientists assigned now, and changes while you add or remove scientists. The real cost of a project is random and hidden - it is not used, so the range is wide; once the spent passes the lower bound the range narrows from below. With no scientists - "no scientists". Display only: the research itself and the saves are not affected (ticket XP-000491).
- HD interface: the windows' buttons, sliders and frames can now have their own colour apart from the battle panel's - new option "HD interface: colour of the windows' buttons" (HD menu, Interface; works with "window buttons in the battle panel's colour" on): "As the battle panel" (as it was), any colour of the panel's list, or "Own colour", made in the "Own colour of the windows" window under it (hue, saturation, brightness, a sample of buttons). So the battle panel can stay "Ship's brass" while the windows are silver or any colour of your own. Only a picture (ticket XP-000479).
- Geoscape: new option "Select markers on the globe with a frame" (HD menu, Geoscape; off by default). Hold the left mouse button on the globe and move the mouse: a frame is drawn, and when you let go, the bases, craft, UFOs and other markers inside it open in the same list a click on a marker gives (a single one - its own window at once). A short click works as always; if the left button is set to rotate the globe, the frame is not used. It only opens the list faster: there is no order to several craft at once (ticket XP-000489).
- Custom colour window: the "-" and "+" buttons at the sliders now change the value by 1 instead of throwing the slider to its end, so a colour can be tuned precisely (ticket XP-000480).
- Production screen: the "Produced" column is moved to the right, the row-reorder arrows no longer cover the first digits of the produced count (ticket XP-000481).
- Soldier transformation window: the soldier-switch arrows ("<<" and ">>" at the top) now also work with the "Previous soldier" / "Next soldier" keys from the options, like in the soldier info window (ticket XP-000483).
- Game log (openxcom.log) is no longer flooded with HD service lines (measurements, picture loading, long frames): they are written only with verbose logging. Warnings and errors stay.
- Lockers next to the base beds no longer show through at the top.
- The wooden farm staircase was redrawn: left and right now share one style, with clean edges and real gaps between the steps.
- Wasteland filling station: the fuel pump and the bucket were redrawn and are no longer blurry. The pump now looks like a real one, with a hose and nozzle; the bucket is round, with even recesses.
- Snow village: the white snow, tiles and garden bed were redrawn from the original's pattern and are sharper; the made-up green and black detail on the bed is gone.
- Pilot voices on the globe: the female pilots and drivers were re-recorded. The old voices sounded masculine despite their pitch, and the Russian lines used the masculine form; now they have a female timbre and the correct wording (ticket XP-000461).
- Launcher 0.4.4: fixed a security issue in the .NET Brotli decoder (CVE-2026-111497); the launcher updates itself.
- Snowy village and winter forest: the grey ground no longer looks like crumpled foil. It was redrawn as fine grain following the original pattern, with three variants per tile so the field does not repeat as a grid, with clean edges; the same ground tiles in the winter forests and in the Ice King village got the same fix (ticket XP-000492).
