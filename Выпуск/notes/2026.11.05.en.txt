- HD interface: every screen can have a window theme of its own - a new row "HD interface: screen themes" (HD menu, Graphics, under the windows' button colour). The screens are in groups: menus, geoscape, base, battle and results, mission messages, landing. Click a group or a screen, pick a colour from the list or your own, the buttons' look and text, the colour of the plain texts ("Text colour": hue and saturation, the brightness and the shadows stay; the buttons' labels are set by "Text of the buttons"); "As the parent" drops its own theme. A screen with no theme takes its group's, a group with none takes the common settings. The themes are kept in a readable file, hd_screen_themes.yml, next to options.cfg; until you set something everything looks as before. The list now has every screen of the game by name: base rooms and their windows, production and its sub-windows, research, craft equipment, options, battle windows (inventory, medikit, scanner, minimap, action menu, briefing and others). Notes, save and load, leaving the game and the options no longer share the geoscape's theme. The battle panel and the battle map stay on their own options.
- Snow village: the bush and the cabbage heads on the garden beds look like plants again instead of stones; the soil and bed tiles are redrawn with fine grain.
- Base, research: with the option "Approximate research term" the scientists window shows "Expected ~N days" under the range, and the research list shows the term in brackets after the number of scientists, for example "3 (2-5d)". It is an estimate from the ruleset cost; the real cost stays hidden.
- The hyena rider no longer walks backwards: as before, the hyena turns before stepping back and pays TU for it. Vehicles keep their reverse gear.
- Sand near the crashed plane (desert and snow, and the "Sands" terrain): the crater is a pit again instead of a frame with pipes, and the flat tile is flat again, with no pipes and no patch among its neighbours.
- Battle map floors: tile edges are now straight instead of stepped, on all flat floors, including the green tiles by the car and the sand. Grass with tufts has only its lower edge straightened.
- Base: on screens with the "<" and ">" arrows, bases can now also be switched with the "Previous unit" and "Next unit" keys, the same way these keys page through soldiers. The left and right arrow keys work as before.
- Battle: the soldier's face on the panel with the weapons is now drawn in high resolution when the look has HD paper doll layers; without them everything stays as before.
