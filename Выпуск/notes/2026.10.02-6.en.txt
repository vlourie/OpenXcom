— The "Apple Processing" add-on (version 1.3) now follows its intended recipe. There are two research topics, both after the Post-Apoc Kitchen: puree takes 72 hours of one scientist, jam 144. Puree is made with Personal Labs and a Workshop: 5 Canteens, 1 Sectoweed and 16 apples give 5 tubes and 1 Hideout Cleaning Bill. Jam is made with an Industrial Printer and a Library: 1 Iron Ore, 25 Potatoes, 1 Sectoweed and 26 apples give 5 jars and 3 Cleaning Bills. A tube still heals like three apples and a jar like five, and they take fewer TUs (5% and 10% less). In exchange they are heavier (by 1 and 3) and restore less morale (by 2 and 3), and jam also restores 2 less energy. Both research topics have their own encyclopedia article with the recipe. If puree is already researched in a campaign, it stays researched.
