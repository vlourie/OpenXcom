- Living base (HD, facility animations): every base facility is painted anew - from the access lift, hangar and labs to the casino, bee hive, dungeon, plantations and defense towers, damaged variants included. The prison is full of cells, a campfire burns in the burrow and a rune blazes in the summoning circle. The base now has day and night by its local time: at night it sinks into blue moonlight and only the lamps, screens and fires of the facilities glow. Lab screens and workshop lights dim when nobody works there, and the living quarters turn their lights off at night. A crew walks the base: scientists go to the labs while research runs, engineers to the workshops, the crew roams the gym, stores and hangars, a guard watches the prison and a nurse stays in the sick bay. At night everyone but the night shift goes to sleep, and those still about carry a lantern. Steam rises over the onsen and the mess hall, the vats and the well bubble, welding sparks fly in the workshops and the forge, embers drift over the burrow's campfire and sparks whirl around the rune. A facility under construction is no longer a grey square: a glowing blue blueprint with scaffolding lies in its place, builders walk over it, and it burns away in patches as the work goes on, revealing the real facility; the days left are still shown. The purple door leaves between rooms no longer cover the painted facilities - only the connecting tube stays on the seam.
- Collapse (option): heaps and blocks now spill. Crumbled debris lands on the free cells beside them - next to a heap about every other cell, next to a block nearly always - so ruins spread out in a skirt of debris instead of a neat grid. The spill does not pass through a standing wall and does not land on units, furniture, map marks or lifts.
- Collapse (option): rubble is dangerous and reshapes the battlefield. A unit caught under a falling ceiling or wall takes a blow to the head - the more fell and the higher it fell from, the harder (up to 70). Under a heap the unit is also pinned and loses all energy. Dust rises over the rubble and blocks sight like smoke for a couple of turns. A heavy pile breaks through a weak upper floor and falls further down. A tree that comes down whole falls to its side and knocks down the crown of its neighbour. Blocks can now be climbed from a heap or crumbled debris next to them - a high point over the ruins - while from flat ground a block still bars the way.
- Battle (HD option "Map during an unseen enemy's turn", on): while an enemy you cannot see moves, the map stays on screen with the camera where you left it instead of the hidden movement screen. Only what your units see is shown. As soon as an enemy comes into view, the camera moves to it as before. Turn the option off to get the old screen back.
- Ufopaedia (HD option "Weapon mode range", on): a weapon's article now has a Range column in its attack table - the range of each firing mode in tiles. Turn the option off for the old table.
- Desert in HD: fallen columns and broken marble slabs in the Atlantean sands are no longer striped smudges - they are pale marble half-buried in sand. The big boulder with a slab leaning on it and the earth ledge with layered sandstone now have HD art, and the red desert gets its own red boulder.
