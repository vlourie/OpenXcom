- Soldier voices: in battle your pirates answer in their own voice when you select them, send them moving, pick up a weapon, or keep poking them. Every soldier type has its own voice - pirates, veterans, freaks, catgirls, gnomes, ogres, syns, soul dolls, chorts and more, and dogs bark; Russian or English lines follow the game language. Slave soldiers, heroes, peasants and damsels can take a voice of their home country at the base in "Voice". Voices for the Repentia, the Lokk'Naar goblin, the catwarrior, the herbalist and the lamia come in the next update; until then these soldiers stay silent.
- Footsteps now depend on footwear - bare feet, boots, heels, heavy armor, dog paws - and on the floor material. Sound only: battle and mechanics are unchanged.
