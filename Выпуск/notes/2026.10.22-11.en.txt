- Battle (HD): two new HD options, "Path number size" and "Path number stroke weight" - the time units and energy numbers on the path tiles can be made smaller and thinner (100% down to 50% in steps of 5%; default 100%, as before). They work when "Path numbers in the interface font" is on (ticket XP-000258).
- Interface (HD): new HD option 'Light up fitting ammo' (off by default) - hover a weapon and the ammunition that fits it is lit: with a green frame in the battle inventory (the floor pile too), with a coloured row in the craft equipment and in the base stores (ticket XP-000257).
