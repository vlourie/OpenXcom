- The 'Wounded in red' option (was 'Craft crew: wounded in red') now also marks the wounded in the base soldier list: the name and 'WOUNDED>days' are red, so you see at once who should not go to training.
- The 'Building collapse' option description now says where it works (most surface maps: towns, villages, farms, junkyards, deserts, forests and jungles) and what the rubble does: debris hits whoever is below, lands as gravel, heaps, boulders and fallen trunks, and raises dust.
- The enemy turn caption now reads 'Enemy turn' instead of 'Enemies are rustling about'.
- New option 'Tree trunk strength' (HD options -> Physics, works with 'Building collapse'): Original, x2, x3 or Mixed. In the original every second trunk falls to a single pistol bullet; x2 and x3 double and triple the trunk armor against everything; Mixed makes bullets and beams barely fell a tree (x3), blasts x1.5, while axes and chainsaws cut as before. Crowns and fire are unchanged.
- Jungle stumps: broken JUNGLESTYX trees now leave HD stumps.
- New separate mod 'Piratez tanks: crush and ram' (enable it in the launcher or the Mods menu): player tanks (autocannon, battle cannon, laser cannon) drive through fences, bushes, light walls and small map objects, breaking them, when that is shorter than going around (object armor up to 60, each broken part adds 4 to the step cost). Doors, mission objectives, base modules, explosive parts and strong walls are left alone. The tank's special weapon button now gives 'Ram': a blow on an enemy or on the wall or object right in front of the tank, 20 TU.
- Craft lights in the base hangars now follow real aviation rules: red on the left wing, green on the right, white at the tail, all steady; a craft ready for take-off also flashes a red beacon (engines about to start). During repairs, rearming and refuelling only the position lights are on, no beacon; with too few pilots aboard the lights are off. Craft parked sideways no longer show red on the right side. Before, a ready craft blinked all its lights green and the other states red.
- New option 'Output through the video card' (HD options -> Speed, off by default): the video card, not the processor, stretches the frame to the window and puts it on the display (OpenGL 3.3). The picture is the same to the pixel. This is the first step of moving the drawing to the video card: the frame itself is still drawn by the processor, so its load does not drop much yet. If the card or its driver cannot do it, the game goes back to the usual output by itself. On laptops with two video cards the game now asks the driver for the powerful one.
