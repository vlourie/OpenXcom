- Weapons in soldiers' hands and items on the floor in HD: all 3096 in-hand frames and 1389 floor frames (bodies included) are upscaled the same way as the soldiers themselves - smooth edges instead of steps, shape and grip exactly as in the original. Tiny things like a grenade in hand barely change: in the original they are a few pixels. Graphics only.
- The last 10 objects in HD walls - a motorbike and a crate at the army base, dark masonry and red roots in desert caves, mossy masonry in the jungle, an orange pipe, concrete slabs, a road sign, a brown cave slope - now have the exact edge of the original: no halo and no gaps at the wall. Graphics only.
- A building collapse off the screen no longer goes unnoticed: if something your soldiers have already seen comes down, a line "Building collapse off the screen" appears at the bottom with the side - left, top right and so on. The camera does not jump; if the collapse is already on the screen or nobody has seen it, there is no line.
- Four new battle panel colours (HD options -> "HD interface: battle panel colour"): "Starship" - lilac alloy on graphite plating, "Niello silver" - on black lacquer, "Black iron and bone" - on charred oak, "Bronze of the deep" - verdigris bronze on copper sheets. For now they are plain panels in their colour; painted keys, backdrop and wells like "Ship's brass" come in the next update. Graphics only.
- HD options: changing any value no longer throws the list back to the top - it stays on the row you were changing. Same when you come back from the detail screen.
- "No saves in battle": leaving a battle is now "Save and abandon game", as in Ironman - the battle is saved to the battle autosave, and you can go on later by loading it from the main menu. Before, leaving dropped the battle unsaved. During the enemy turn and while an action plays out the button is hidden, as in Ironman.
- Hills in the forest (green, snowy and scorched) and gravel between the rocks in Martian caves no longer look like tiles with a stripe at every seam in HD: the slope runs as one piece, with the colour and shading of the original. Graphics only.
