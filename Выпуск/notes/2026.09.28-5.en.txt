— Craft lights in the hangars now show the craft's state. Ready to launch: blinking green. Under repair: an even red pulse. Rearming: two quick red flashes and a pause. Not enough pilots aboard: red lights blink on the left and right side in turn. Refuelling: only the beacons, as before. Each hangar also has a garland of five bulbs along its bottom edge showing the fuel: up to 25% one red, up to 50% two orange, up to 75% three yellow, up to 99% four green, and a full tank lights all five, alternating green and gold. Works when "Craft animations" is on in the HD settings.
— If "Base facility animations" is turned off in the HD settings, base facilities look as they used to, in the classic style without HD pictures. Before, the HD pictures stayed on the base screen, only frozen. The setting takes effect after restarting the game, and its tooltip now says so.
— The globe has a scale of its own, apart from the windows, the base and the lists: Options → HD → "HD interface: globe scale". Keep a big interface (Geoscape Scale 5x or 6x in the Video options) and still get a detailed globe, as at 3x: the land, its textures and the day-night line are drawn finer, while buttons, labels and markers keep their size. The default, "As geoscape", changes nothing. Works with the HD interface on.
