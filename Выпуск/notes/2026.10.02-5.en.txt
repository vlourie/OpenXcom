— Three X-Piratez add-ons can now be installed from the launcher as separate ticks (Settings → Game components…): "Piratez tanks: turning turret" - the pirate tanks' turret turns apart from the hull; "Apple Processing" - apple puree (three apples in one action) and apple jam (five); "Kisya and Brysya Sisters" - the catgirl Puma Sisters return to the campaign. None of them switches on by itself, only when ticked. Each is described on the site, under Mods.
— "Piratez tanks: turning turret" (version 1.3): the tank no longer has a second turret that stays fixed. In HD the old hull with its painted-on gun was drawn over the new one; the mod now brings HD frames of its own, the hull and every turret position sharp and in the same colours. Only the picture: the tank shoots and moves as before.
— The "Do not follow projectiles with the camera" setting at value 2 works as described again: the camera does not fly after anyone's shots, yours or the enemy's. In previous versions this value followed every shot, like 0. The setting's hint now also describes values 3 (like 2, but the camera centres on a hit on your soldier or a civilian) and 4 (like 3, but the camera does not move at all - this is what gentle mode uses). Only the camera changes: shots, hits and turns are the same.
