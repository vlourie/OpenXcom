- "May collapse" hint in the aim: when you aim a grenade, a rocket or another explosion, the tiles where part of a building may come down after it are marked with yellow and black hazard stripes over the blast area. It is worked out from the strongest damage the explosion can do, hence "may": a miss or a weak throw brings down less. Only what your soldiers have already seen is marked; if a level above the one you look at comes down, the mark is on your level. HD modes only, with building collapse and the blast area on.
- Desert dunes, snowy polar hills, rock blocks and slopes in underwater caves and the steps of the jungle temple no longer look like tiles with a stripe at every seam in HD: the slope runs as one piece, with the colour and shading of the original. Graphics only.
