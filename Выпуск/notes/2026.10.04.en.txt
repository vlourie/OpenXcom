- The craft equipment screen has two new buttons at the bottom, "Load" and "Empty". "Load" puts everything currently shown in the list onto the craft while there is room, "Empty" takes everything shown back to the stores. Both follow the selected filter and the quick search: pick, say, "Ammo" and press "Load". Vehicles and turrets are left alone by "Load": they take soldier seats, so load them with the arrows. The buttons work both in the campaign and in New Battle.
