— The launcher has a Voice section (launcher 0.3.7): voice rooms with your friends from the site, push-to-talk while you play, the launcher can sit in the tray, and a room link on the site opens it in the launcher. Voice is in a closed test for now: for other players the section says that voice is not available yet. We will tell you when it opens for everyone.
— The HD interface fonts now live in a separate mod, "HD core: fonts", and the X-Piratez HD mod is called "HD graphics: X-Piratez". Nothing to do: if you have HD, this update brings the new mod, and the launcher switches it on before the HD mod in every build where HD is on. The interface looks as before. This update is installed by launcher 0.3.7, so the launcher updates itself first.
— The setup wizard ("Settings → Game components…") no longer removes anything from an existing installation on its own. Its checkboxes follow what you already have installed. If you untick something installed, the wizard shows what will be removed and asks you to confirm.
— After a launcher update, the shortcuts on the desktop and in the Start menu show the new icon right away. Before, the old icon stayed until you refreshed the desktop by hand. If there were no shortcuts (the launcher was unpacked from an archive), they are created once after the update.
