- Launcher 0.3.19, "New build": you can now pick the game - besides X-Piratez, six game mods from mod.io: The X-Com Files, Warhammer 40k, ROSIGMA (installed on top of 40k), X-Chronicles, Mars-com and The World of Terrifying Silence (it needs the Terror from the Deep data - the launcher finds it in Steam or GOG and copies it itself). The launcher asks first, downloads the game straight from mod.io with its author and version, checks the file and installs it as a build of its own; your X-Piratez saves are not touched.
- Launcher 0.3.19: updates are smaller and faster. An update now comes as one compressed file instead of thousands of separate ones (resumed if the connection drops): a typical release with a new engine is about 10 MB instead of 61. The release file list read at every launcher start is 5 MB instead of 20.
