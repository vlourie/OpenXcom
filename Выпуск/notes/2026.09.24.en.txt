The first full HD release: our own graphics, our own launcher and our own site.

Graphics
— HD battlescape art: cursors, the reticle, path arrows, status icons over bodies, bullet tracers.
— The interface font is picked from eight faces in the options and changes without a restart.
— Every HD option now lives on its own "HD" tab instead of being scattered through "Advanced".
— On start the game asks for the language and the art version: 18+ or classic. The answer is never remembered, so nothing slips out on stream.

Launcher
— Downloads and verifies updates on its own, repairs damaged files and rolls a bad update back. It never touches your saves, options or your own mods.
— A "Graphics" tab: every HD picture is shown next to the original, and you can say whether it is good enough. Floors are shown as a field of map tiles, not as a single tile.
— The launcher links to your account on the site with a short code — no password typed anywhere.
— A "Support ♥" button: for the author of X-Piratez and for the HD team.

Problem reports
— F8 in game: pause, a screenshot and a report form. The log and the save are attached only if you tick them, and paths with your user name are stripped from the log.
— With no internet the report is not lost: it waits in the launcher and goes with one button. "My reports" in the main menu shows the stage and the team's answer.

Site
— The wiki is built from the mod's own files: 13,563 X-Piratez pages, with the same numbers the game uses. Search works in Russian and English.
— A forum and a support desk: a ticket can be written without signing up.

Fixes
— Descenders (p, y, g, q) are no longer cut off at the bottom of a line.
— Numbers are back in the Bootypedia instead of question marks.
— Recoloured soldiers no longer turn purple.
— Globe labels are drawn with the crisp font; the globe itself spins almost twice as smoothly.
— The end-of-turn screen and the enemy turn no longer stall.
